## Translation rules

- No summarizing: represent every substantive narrative paragraph, dialogue unit, quotation, verse/list and rhetorical question.
- Preserve agency, emotional force, irony, humour, repetition, insults, religious/caste/social language and period rhetoric without softening or intensifying them.
- Do not beautify into contemporary literary English or add modern explanations.
- Preserve source oddities and verified discontinuities instead of inferring missing Tamil.
- Preserve chapter alignment exactly; mixed scans split only at centered source headings.
- Use stable readable romanization without diacritics unless an established English form is clearly preferable; preserve full/short name distinctions.
- Use direct English equivalents where safe and retain culture/period terms when flattening would lose source character.
- Spoken dialogue uses standard English double quotation marks; embedded quotation uses single quotation marks.
- Keep source/provenance HTML comments at meaningful scan boundaries and verified joins.
