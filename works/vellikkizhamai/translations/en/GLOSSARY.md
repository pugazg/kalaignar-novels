## Weekday wordplay policy

Chapter 1 deliberately jokes about weekday names and sayings. Preserve the joke rather than replacing it with an unrelated English proverb.

- `செவ்வாயோ வெறுவாயோ` → **“Tuesday—or an empty mouth!”** in the pilot;
- `பொன் அகப்பட்டாலும் புதன் அகப்படாது` → **“You may get hold of gold, but never of Wednesday.”**

The following sentence about sleeping Tuesday night and waking Thursday morning is retained because it explains the narrator’s joke. Do not modernize these sayings into contemporary English idioms.

## Style lock after pilot review

The Chapter 1 pilot establishes these continuing conventions:

1. readable but source-bound English; no summarizing;
2. preserve rhetorical questions, repeated exclamations, abrupt turns and comic repetition;
3. preserve transitions between first-person interior monologue and third-person narration;
4. standard English double quotation marks for speech, single quotation marks for quotations within speech;
5. preserve paragraph units unless English syntax demands only a minimal adjustment;
6. source joins remain reversible with HTML comments;
7. source page markers remain in chapter files at meaningful scan boundaries;
8. do not add explanatory theology, caste history, folklore interpretation or modern social commentary inside translated prose;
9. retain source strangeness where certainty is limited; document rather than silently repair;
10. translated embedded religious verse follows only the wording printed in the source edition.
