## 6. Translation style lock

Across Batches 1–4 the following remain locked unless the whole-work bilingual review documents a specific correction:

- no summarising or substantive omission;
- natural English syntax only where source meaning/agency remains unchanged;
- preserve speaker boundaries, responsibility, repetition, rhetoric and emotional force;
- do not soften or intensify political/religious/caste/social rhetoric;
- preserve source-period and gendered/social framing as the author's rhetoric rather than silently modernizing it;
- do not invent explanations inside prose;
- prefer conservative transliteration when lexical/cultural force would otherwise be over-interpreted;
- preserve recurring **bud / leaf** imagery through the ending;
- preserve direct emotional `Appa` / `Amma` register where established;
- retain reversible scan/page provenance in HTML comments;
- do not invent source-backed chapter divisions.
