## Translation policy

- no summarising or substantive omission;
- natural English syntax only where source meaning and agency remain unchanged;
- preserve speaker boundaries, responsibility, repetition, rhetoric and emotional force;
- do not soften/intensify political, religious, caste or social rhetoric;
- preserve source-period and gendered/social framing as the author's rhetoric rather than modernizing it;
- do not invent explanations inside prose;
- prefer conservative transliteration when lexical/cultural force would otherwise be over-interpreted;
- preserve recurring **bud / leaf** imagery through the ending;
- retain reversible scan/page provenance;
- do not invent chapters or an ending marker.
