## Locked style conventions

Batches 1–2 establish and preserve these conventions:

- final English is readable but source-bound; no sentence-level summarising;
- source paragraph and speaker boundaries are preserved;
- source ellipses, emphatic repetition and rhetorical punctuation remain visible where they carry force;
- English syntax may be naturalized without adding motive, explanation or historical identification absent from Tamil;
- cultural terms are translated functionally only when source meaning is clear; otherwise conservative transliteration is preferred;
- direct emotional kin terms may remain transliterated when that preserves voice (`Appa`, `Paatti`), while narration uses ordinary English kinship terms;
- source metaphors are retained even when unusual in English, including `worm in embers`, the child's `dream world`, and Kumar's `scales`;
- scan/page provenance remains in HTML comments;
- cross-page source fragments may be translated as one natural English unit only when the Tamil audit already established the join;
- the scan 13 / printed 8 → scan 14 / printed 10 jump remains explicit; no printed page 9 is inferred.

Batch 3 may add new terms but must preserve these locked conventions unless a documented contradiction with the audited source requires review.
