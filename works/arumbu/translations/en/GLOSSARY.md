## Pilot style lock

Batch 1 established these general conventions:

- final English is readable but source-bound; no sentence-level summarising;
- source paragraph and speaker boundaries are preserved;
- source ellipses, emphatic repetition and rhetorical punctuation remain visible where they carry force;
- English syntax may be naturalized without adding motive, explanation or historical identification absent from Tamil;
- cultural terms are translated functionally only when the source meaning is clear; otherwise conservative transliteration is preferred;
- scan/page provenance remains in HTML comments;
- cross-page source fragments may be translated as one natural English unit only when the Tamil audit already established the join.

Batch 2 may add new terms but must preserve these locked pilot conventions unless a documented contradiction with the audited source requires review.
