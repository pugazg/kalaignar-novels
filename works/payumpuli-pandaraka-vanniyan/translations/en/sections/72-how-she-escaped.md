# How She Escaped!

Kuruvichchi had imprisoned Piyaseeli, still in her Kurathi disguise, in a room of the Mullaitheevu palace, told four guards to remain inside that room and guard her securely, and then hurried away to see Pandaraka Vanniyan. Kurathi Piyaseeli's eyes circled around the room.

“What are you looking at? Are you checking whether there is some way to escape from this room?”

A thick-moustached guard asked the question while staring at Piyaseeli as though threatening her.

“Chee! Chee! Why should I run away? I have come here as Pandaraka Vanniyan's guest. Would I have the heart to flee from here?”

“Then why that eagle-eyed look?”

“I was only thinking that I am the only woman in such a beautiful room—and there are four of you.”

“Even our names are Raman, Ilakkuvan, Bharathan and Sathrukkanan! See how well the names of the four of us match?”

“I do not like the Ramayana! It is the Mahabharata that I—

—like!”

“Then?”

“If there were five of you, and those five were named Dharman, Bheeman, Archunan, Nakulan and Sahadevan, how delighted this Panchali would have been! Never mind! Nothing terrible happens because one is missing! Leave that aside. In such a spacious room there is only one little bed! Should there not be a bed as large as a playing field for all five of us to lie down and roll about on?”

“What nonsense are you talking, woman? Do not forget that you are a prisoner locked up here after being accused of coming to betray this land!”

“Tell the truth! Am I not now imprisoned in the heart of every one of you? Touch your hearts and tell me!”

As Piyaseeli said this, sparkling and flashing, one of the four bared his teeth in a grin and twisted about. The thick-moustached man noticed and quickly slapped him on the back to bring him under control.

Catching this from the corner of her eye, Piyaseeli looked at the thick-moustached man. “What, sir! Perhaps you are not a man, but is it fair to deny that man even the freedom to bare his teeth?” she said, giving his shoulder a little pat.

At the touch of those soft fingers, many mighty warriors and commanders had fallen for her as though an iron ball had dropped into the middle of the sea. But the thick-moustached man and two of the other guards looked at her with boundless disgust, as though looking at a poisonous insect. That one man—

<!-- source boundary: scan 357 → scan 358 -->

—alone bared his teeth and writhed; then, afraid that both rows of his teeth would come loose if he disobeyed the chief guard any further, he pretended to look at her angrily. Yet Piyaseeli did not fail to notice saliva trickling from the corner of his lips. She smiled with the pride that one out of four had fallen into her grasp.

“All right! All right! Pull that curtain across, hide the bed and lie down to sleep. We will stay inside this room and keep guard,” said the thick-moustached man.

“Do none of you have even a little decency? How can a woman lie down alone and sleep in front of so many men? Suppose the cloth on my body slips aside in my sleep! Must I close my eyes while giving you the sight of a half-clad beauty?”

“That is why we are telling you to pull the curtain across.”

“Does any of you know how to massage feet?”

“We know how to seize a throat!”

“Ayyo! Are you murderous sinners?”

“Stop talking! Go and lie down properly!”

“How can I sleep? From the beating your queen, Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar, gave me—and from being thrown down and rolled on the ground—my whole body is bruised. I will go to the bathing room, pour some hot water over myself, and come back.”

As she spoke, Piyaseeli began removing her clothes one by one in the midst of the four men. All four closed their eyes. They had closed their eyes, yes—but except for the thick-moustached man, the other—

<!-- source boundary: scan 358 → scan 359 -->

—three left a tiny slit between their eyelids and enjoyed watching the grace of her beauty. In her haste to remove her clothes, a long iron key tucked at her waist fell to the floor.

“What is such a large iron key for?”

“This? This key is not for opening any cash box!”

“Then what is it for?”

“Sometimes I suffer a terrible *kakka-valippu* seizure. At such times, if someone puts this iron key into my hand, the seizure gradually subsides. That is why I keep it.”

The thick-moustached man's face revealed a sudden worry arising in his heart—what a pity, that such a beautiful young woman should have such an illness—and then immediately disappearing. Piyaseeli noticed both that change and the way he mastered it and turned his face away. She smiled.

She stripped off three-quarters of the clothes that had covered her body—and had covered it only partially to begin with. Wearing only a small piece of cloth above and a short skirt at her waist, she said hurriedly, “All right! All right! I am going to bathe,” entered the bathing room, and suddenly fell down with a cry. All four guards stared in shock. Lying on her back on the floor, she flung her arms and legs about and rolled from side to side. Her mouth too twisted and pulled crookedly.

“Oh! It is the *kakka-valippu* she told us about,” the thick-moustached man decided. The guard who had bared his teeth now seemed intent on showing that all thirty-two of them were still in place. The thick-moustached man glared at the three guards and told them to go outside—

<!-- source boundary: scan 359 → scan 360 -->

—and they went out reluctantly. Turning his face in another direction, the thick-moustached man picked up the key from the floor and thrust it into Piyaseeli's hand. Then he too went outside and stood with the other three guards. He stood resolutely without even looking inside, like some sage performing penance. But the faces of the other three, almost without their realizing it, kept peering into the room. The thick-moustached man was not prepared to allow that. He closed the room door and stood outside with the other three, keeping very alert guard.

Piyaseeli, who had been rolling about naked as at birth, glanced toward the door from the corner of her eye and let out a deep breath. She lay quietly on the floor, still completely naked. The thick-moustached man slowly opened the door and looked in.

“The seizure has passed! She is unconscious,” he muttered.

Believing that Piyaseeli was lying inside in a dishevelled state, they continued guarding the room from outside. But she opened the rear door of the room and escaped.

When Piyaseeli finished telling Lord North the story of how she had escaped, North cried, “Bravo! Bravo! What a clever woman!” He lifted her up and leapt about that luxurious room like a demon. She held tightly to his shoulders so that she would not fall, laughing aloud.
