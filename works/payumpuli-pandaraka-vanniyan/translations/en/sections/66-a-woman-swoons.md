# A Woman Swoons!

Surrounded by many hundreds, Pandaraka Vanniyan's army set out. At the head of the force, mounted on two horses, rode Pandarakan Vairamuthu and Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar—the virtuous woman who filled his loving heart like a wave reaching the shore. Their eyes had become pieces of burning ember, and they led the army with swords raised high in their hands.

“Straight to Kakkai Vanniyan's fort, isn't it?” Kuruvichchi asked, seething.

“No! First to the Vattrappalai Kannagiyamman temple! I must see with my own eyes what has happened to Thanigaimala there. Only after that do we attack Kakkai Vanniyan's fort!”

Pandaraka Vanniyan roared.

Inside Kakkai Vanniyan's fort, in that marble bedroom arranged like a dance hall of *Kaamavel*, Nalla Naachchi paced with her hands bound. The moment she heard the doors slowly creak, she stopped and turned. Kakkai Vanniyan entered dressed in gleaming silk, adorned with ornaments set with shining nine-gem jewels, while attar, *punugu* and *javvadu* all scented his body at once.

“Well, dear—is every comfort properly arranged? Did you eat the fruit? Did you drink the milk?”

The little parrot gave no answer to Kakkai's question.

“Oh! Were you waiting until I came so the two of us could drink together? Yes, that is right too! You can take a fruit, peel it and feed the segments into my mouth—and I can wet the rim of the milk bowl with my lips and place it against yours—ah! That is a pleasure all its own! You will not have had that experience! But I do not know what merit a bedroom like this performed in some birth, to take form as Kakkai Vanniyan's bedroom and become accustomed to seeing and hearing the games of many kinds of women, their naked forms, and the cries that emerge in the pleasure of delight!
