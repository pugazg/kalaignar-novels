# An Unmatched Pair!

To dear Vairamuthu, affectionate greetings from your friend Kannusami. It feels as though four ages have passed since separation came between us. My royal duties and your work of struggle have dug between us a trench called separation. Here Minister Pilimathala, as usual, continues circling me with a deceitful heart. On top of that, since his beloved daughter Piyasili—who had been made my wife—died and was gone, another grief has been gnawing at him. His heart is now like a house already ruined by a lightning strike, with rats burrowing into every corner! Yet in that dark heart there is no shortage of blind schemes. It is perfectly clear that the British governor, that power-mad and arrogant Lord North, has filled Pilimathala with confidence. Thinking that I know nothing, the spies North sends wander through Kandy trying to turn the people's thoughts in other directions, and they also meet Pilimathala secretly again and again. Their work is like that *neruppu-kkozhi* which hides only its head and imagines its entire body is concealed!

<!-- source boundary: scan 238 → scan 239 -->

Our spies are skilled workers, so they pick those men out as one picks stones from rice. Then they keep following them. At high noon our shadow does not fall away upon the ground; it seems to spread directly over us. Like that shadow, our spies follow the enemy's spies. You know that one division of my spy force is stationed in the capital city of Colombo itself! Through this letter I am introducing to you two talented people from that division who have worked intensely in the Colombo city area. Jeyaseelan and Marthani are husband and wife. You will already have understood: the husband is mute! His wife Marthani, however, is a chatterbox who can speak enough for him as well! But both are experts in the art of investigation! I have sent such a pair to Mullaitheevu so that they may assist you.

Like the wind, those two entered even Lord North's fort in Colombo and swept up so much information that they left me astonished! The highly secret documents they gave me—the conspiracy plans devised by the British—are extremely important. It seems to me that it would be very easy to send our forces into the capital of Colombo itself and arrest Lord North, like entering a tiger's own cave to hunt it. Jeyaseelan and Marthani have given me every route and method for doing so. I am planning like a circling eagle. One single pounce! A fat goat—yes! Lord North, who now rules the roost in Colombo, is going to be caught by me and die! The information Jeyaseelan and Marthani have supplied has given me that much confidence!

<!-- source boundary: scan 239 → scan 240 -->

Do not dismiss Jeyaseelan merely because he is mute! Do not regard Marthani lightly merely because she is a woman!

Through this letter I must tell you about one adventure they carried out in Colombo.

They say Lord North wants a different woman every day—to sit with him and drink, and to lie with him and take pleasure! They say he waits yearning for night to come! They say he has grown tired of white women as “domestic stock”! So his lackeys cast their nets among the young women of Colombo! Many women with nothing, tempted by the high price, sold their bodies for a night and received gold and jewels! They say even some elegant, fashionable women from wealthy households thought that rolling upon the powerful man's *amsa-thoolika* bed would raise their status, and ran to him before they were even invited! Then one day, in those lustful entertainments that had become a daily occurrence, there was suddenly a famine! Lord North's messengers wandered about like dogs—but found no fresh “stock”! Many women came forward, but they were women already known to him. The English lord's son wanted someone new every day!

At last the eyes of those wandering men fell upon Marthani! That is to say, she had deliberately behaved so that their eyes would fall upon her! The lord's lackeys cast their net for her! Marthani, eager to enter the enemy camp and pretending to be caught in the net—would she refuse? She placed only one condition: her mute husband must come with her! The messengers agreed. They carried both of them off to the women's quarters of the Colombo fort!
