# North and Treachery!

Unable to refuse Vikrama Rajasingan's request that he remain in Kandy until Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar had completely recovered, Pandaraka Vanniyan postponed his journey and stayed with Kuruvichchi in his dear friend's palace.

He sent word that it would take him several more days to return, and that until then his sisters Nallanaachchi and Oomaichchi Naachchi need not wait at the Mullaitheevu palace. They could return instead to the regions under their supervision—Nallanaachchi to Trincomalee and Oomaichchi Naachchi to Panangamam.

Unable to understand why Pandaraka Vanniyan was remaining in Kandy without returning to Mullaitheevu, the two sisters departed in confusion: Nallanaachchi returned to Trincomalee and Oomaichchi Naachchi to Panangamam to attend to their responsibilities.

Meanwhile, no one knew where Kandy's minister Pilimathala had gone. The news created great commotion in the Kandy palace. Both Vikrama Rajasingan and Pandaraka Vanniyan were greatly surprised that Pilimathala had suddenly left Kandy.

Where would schemers like Pilimathala—men with treacherous minds—go? To the enemy's abode, of course!

<!-- source boundary: scan 179 → scan 180 -->

That treacherous viper went and fell at the feet of Governor North, enthroned in the capital of Colombo.

He wailed that the plan he had devised after thinking until the very venom of his brain seemed wrung dry had not only failed, but had left Pandaraka Vanniyan and Vikrama Rajasingan bound in an even deeper friendship. What was he to do now?

North spoke to him with confidence.

“Do not underestimate British statecraft or military strength! Muthusami, whose stomach burns because he cannot bear to see Kannusami as king of Kandy, is in my hands. From here in Colombo, a proclamation is going to be made declaring Muthusami himself king of Kandy!”

“Lord North! If you announce this, will not the people of the kingdom of Kandy rise in furious revolt? Will not that upheaval and confusion give Vikrama Rajasingan even greater strength? Have you considered that?”

North, who had not expected this question from Pilimathala, patted him approvingly.

“Bravo! Exactly the right question! But it is not something I failed to consider. I came to this decision only after thinking it through thoroughly. First, Pilimathala must return to Kandy. If Vikrama Rajasingan or Pandaraka Vanniyan asks about your visit to Colombo, do you know what you must say?” he asked, laughing, smoking and drinking.
