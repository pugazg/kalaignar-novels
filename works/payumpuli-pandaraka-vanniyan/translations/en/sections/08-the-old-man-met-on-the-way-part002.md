Seeing Sundaralingam, the old man asked, “Who is it?” and sat up a little. Perhaps because he had moved, he was seized by a cough he could not control. Realizing that the old man was in great discomfort, Sundaralingam rubbed his chest and also took the water kept in a vessel on the veranda and gave it to him to drink. After catching his breath, the old man looked at him and asked, “Who are you, lad? What do you want?”

“I come from Tamil Nadu, Grandfather! I have an important task at Pandaraka Vanniyan's palace.”

“Is that so? I'm very glad!”

“Grandfather! On my way here I passed two or three villages, and now I see this village too. The whole place is dressed for a festival. But there isn't a person moving about. Why is that?”

“Oh, that? Today is the final day of the Visaka festival! There will be celebrations all day at the Vattrappalai Kannagi Amman temple. Everyone in this area will be at the Vattrappalai Kannagi temple. Only helpless old men like me are left lying at home. It is for the Visaka festival that we have tied banana plants and festoons at every house. And this year, because Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar herself is leading the Vattrappalai Visaka festival, it will be especially grand! I alone have not been fortunate enough to see it.”

“Who is Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar, Grandfather?”

“King Pandaraka Vanniyan, you know, is very young! In strength of heart, no one

can equal him! Ever since he assumed responsibility as king of this region, it has always been a battlefield! Struggle itself is his life! All his close relatives have tried again and again to persuade such a man to marry. Do you know the condition he laid down?”

“What, Grandfather? What condition did he lay down?”

“He told them firmly, ‘Until we drive out the domination of the whites and safeguard the freedom of this land, I do not want to marry. Whoever the woman who wishes to marry me may be, she must stand with me from this very moment and support me in the struggle against white domination!’”

As soon as the old man gave him this information, Sundaralingam's heart began to pound faster. He showed an eager restlessness to learn more. The old man continued.

“Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar is the woman who has come forward saying she is ready for that. Pandaraka Vanniyan has said that he will marry Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar only after his goal is fully achieved—and for now he has accepted her as his beloved! Because he has accepted her as his beloved, Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar is conducting the Visaka festival at the Vattrappalai Kannagi temple and fulfilling her prayer to Kannagi Amman. People say the king too may come to the festival.”
After explaining this, the old man began to cough again because he had spoken for so long.

Sundaralingam rubbed his chest and made him recline there on the veranda. Clearing his throat and struggling to bring the cough under control, the old man looked at Sundar<!-- verified source split-word boundary: scan 32 → scan 33 within Sundaralingam -->alingam and said, “You still haven't told me who you are! You only said ‘Tamil Nadu’—just that, bare.”

“Isn't Tamil Nadu itself bare today, Grandfather!” he replied in despair.

“Why do you say that? Isn't it a land of abundance?”

“It is a land of abundance! But today, isn't the flag of foreign domination beginning to fly over it! Rights are being destroyed! The feeling that they must be recovered is slowly withering away! There is no unity among Tamils! There is no sense of self-respect! Traitors who grin for the pleasures of office after betraying those who are ready to fight for rights—and to give their lives for them—are multiplying.”

The old man's eyes filled with tears. Some emotion caught in his throat!

“Yes, brother—it's the same here too! Only our Pandaraka Vanniyan stands opposed to the domination of the *Parangiyars*. The others have knelt down! But in this region—among the people of Mullaitheevu—ninety out of a hundred stand solidly behind Pandaraka Vanniyan, people like us.”

“It's the same situation in Tamil Nadu! I am going to Pandaraka Vanniyan's palace precisely to explain that situation, learn the situation here, and return.”

“You still haven't told me your name!”

“My name is Sundaralingam! I am a commander in Kattabomman's army at Panchalankurichi!”

“Oh! Are you that lion of the oppressed community? I have heard that you and Vellaiyathevan are like brothers.”

“Is that so? Grandfather, are you well acquainted with affairs in Tamil Nadu too? Do you have connections with the palace here?”

“I did once! I was a commander—look here! I lost a leg in a battle!”

The old man pulled aside the blanket covering his leg and showed him. Sundaralingam began to feel a special respect for the old man.

“Grandfather! May I know your name?”

“They call me Sangili Thalapathi! Now they call me Sangili Thatha!”

“Does Sangili Thatha have any grandsons or granddaughters?”

“No grandson! This grandfather has only one granddaughter, who has lost both her mother and father! Do you know what that granddaughter's name is?”

“What is it, Grandfather?”

“Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar!”
