# Note

Was Pandaraka Vanniyan’s death a natural one? Or did he die on the battlefield? History does not state this clearly. But one thing is certain: until the very end, he continued to fight against the foreigners. Therefore I ask readers to read once again, as the conclusion to this story, the “Preface” I wrote for this serial narrative.

M. K.
