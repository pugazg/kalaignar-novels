# This Is One of Them!

The sound of Piyaseeli's laughter rang out like music! It throbbed like a drum! It rose like a fiddle! Amid that riot of music, North began dancing even while holding her in the position in which he had seized her. She too clung tightly around his neck and showered kiss after kiss upon him like rain. The mischief of the liquor they had drunk flew like a banner, carrying them toward the very peak of their sensual revelry!

Still dancing, still holding her tightly, he asked whether she would empty one more bottle. Her words slurring, she replied—

“My darling! Give me into my mouth the saliva that flows from yours! It will be terribly, terribly intoxicating! You have drunk so much, so give me your spit, my lord! My god! That is the finest liquor now! That is what will make me reel the most!”

On hearing this answer from Piyaseeli, he fulfilled her request with the dizziness of a man who had drunk ten more cups of liquor.

“What do you want in return, my king?” she asked, thinking she was embracing him with her arms while winding her legs around his body. As he lowered her to the floor, he said, “I want to suck your blo—

—od and drink it. Liquor mixed with this beauty's blood—ah, how delicious that would be!” He laughed. It did not sound like an ordinary laugh. It was a crazed laugh. Piyaseeli was frightened. She rolled about on the floor and moved a little distance away. He too rolled after her, embraced her, pinned her down and bent her to his hold. Deep in drunkenness, Piyaseeli stammered, “My lord! How can you suck my blood?” Even then she was still embracing him. Neither of them knew what they were doing. The final scene of that frenzy of lust became a very “Rudra Tandavam”!

“You ask how I can suck it? You mad girl! The way your Krishna Paramatma sucked Poothaki's blood—that is how I am going to suck yours!”

Laughing wildly, he kissed her breast with brutal force. “Oh! Only a kiss?” Piyaseeli said, and began to savour the pleasure.

Though she sensed that this embrace was different from the many embraces of that night, she went along with him as though she desired it. Did she go along with him? No! Such was the tyranny of the liquor that had brought her under its control. There was no part of her body that North's teeth did not touch. The marks of his teeth and nails were not tender memories that she could admire the next morning in the bath. Instead, like red stones peeping out from worked gold, bloody wounds sprang rapidly across her mango-hued body. Like round young drops of dew resting at dawn on arugampul grass—but

red—drops of blood covered her body! Though in the intoxication of pleasure and liquor she did not fully feel the pain of those bites, she shuddered crying “Ah! Ooh!!”, then embraced him again and rolled with him on the floor.

To satisfy her own desire she too bit and tasted him in one or two places. But his manner became increasingly savage.

“My delight! What is this—why am I receiving such pleasure? Today I am getting a pleasure I have never known before! Your mirror-like cheek! Ayyo! Ayyo! I can see my face in your cheek!”

Piyaseeli's cheek became wounded. “Ah! What are you doing?” she whimpered, yet in that stupor in which pain did not register, she only held him tighter.

“I want to bite you just like this, to suck you like a mango, until every last bit of my desire is satisfied!”

Saying this, he began kissing her breast again. At first she lay there enjoying it—then she began to scream. He would not let go. The more she cried out, the more his frenzy rose instead of subsiding.

“Let me go! Let me go! Let go! I cannot bear the pain! I will die!”

She pleaded with him. He was tasting her out of desire alone. He bore her no anger at all. When a squirrel gets a guava in its hands, does it bite and tear the fruit apart because it is angry with the fruit? That was how he tore into that guava. Piyaseeli wailed! Begged! Screamed! She seized his head and struggled to shake him away. But he went on calling her honey—milk—heaven itself—nectar impossible to find—

and, as though determined to force her body into his mouth and swallow her whole, revelled in a wild frenzy.

That battle went on for a long time. She struggled like a goat caught in the jaws of a tiger. No pity arose in him. Delirium! Delirium! His uncontrollable desire for her was like letting a spotted deer leap before a starving tiger. She was a woman who liked to taste a little of such roughness now and then. Yet this roughness suddenly changed—not into the roughness a young bull might show a heifer, but into the violence of a rhinoceros—and she was terrified.

Realizing that Lord North had completely lost himself, and with the pain of wounds across her body somewhat reducing her own intoxication, she looked at him and pleaded, “Please let me go!”

She who had grown used to begging broad-shouldered men for desire—“Please embrace me!” and “Please don't let me go! Hold me!”—now for the first time looked at Lord North with tears in her eyes and cried, “Please, let me go!”

Poor woman—if only he had still been in this world! He had by then crossed beyond the very shores of the ocean of pleasure.

Suddenly came a demonic scream. After that no sound came from Piyaseeli. Yet his kissing did not stop. Until dawn he went on devouring her. At last, unable to continue, he rolled aside and lay down. He did not even know that he had done so. Only after daybreak would Lord North discover beside him the corpse of the great beauty Piyaseeli.

She had realized with her own eyes that among all worldly pleasures, the highest was the pleasure born in the dance hall of the god of love; she had believed that immersing herself in that pleasure was the purpose of birth, the aim of life and the very nature of womanhood. Leaping into men's laps and delighting in their grasp, that mad young woman's life ended—just as she had wished—in some man's bedchamber.

The dances she danced—the races she ran—the songs she sang—the men she sought—all of it, pain within pleasure and ruin within delight, mingled together and came to an end.

When Lord North's stupor cleared, he wrung his hands and cried, “What have I done!” The officers he summoned came running and said, “There is no time to sit worrying. Let us see what must be done now!”

As planned, the coffin arrived. As though placing a golden creeper studded with gems inside a casket and closing it, they laid that golden-statue-like woman, now covered in drops of blood, inside the coffin and shut it.

Many had now finished reading a tragic epic and closed the cover of the book. History holds any number of examples showing that this is how those who bewitch others may meet their end. This is one of them!
