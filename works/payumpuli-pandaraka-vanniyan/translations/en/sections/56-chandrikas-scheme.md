# Chandrika's Scheme!

“Are you the mute Jeyaseelan's younger sister?”

“Yes! You seem to know everything about me!”

“Ask respectfully, ‘How do you know all that?’”

“That's simply how I'm used to speaking. Don't you know how a Kurathi woman speaks?”

“Are you a Kurathi by lineage?”

“You know nothing about my lineage. Nor is there any need for you to know it. It is enough for you to know only that I am Kala, Jeyaseelan's younger sister. If you want one more piece of news, I will tell you. For the royal duty I have undertaken, I am in the guise of a Kurathi woman.”

“For how long?”

“From the very day the king of Kandy's command was given to me!”

“Then tell me—you have just been to Kakkai Vanniyan's fort and returned, haven't you?”

“Did my brother Jeyaseelan tell you even that?”

“His wife Marthani told me all the details!”

“Marthani! Ah! What a jewel among women she is! A sacred lamp of chastity! To her, her husband is the god she sees before her own eyes!”

<!-- source boundary: scan 273 → scan 274 -->

As Kala sang Marthani's saga, Thanigaimala laughed inwardly. For he was the man who had lain in the lap of that jewel among women—the man caught in her grasp—the man who had extinguished that sacred lamp—the man worshipped by that deity! And so he laughed to himself.

“Did you find any clue in Kakkai Vanniyan's mansion?”

“He truly believed I was a Kurathi fortune-teller. I held his hand and told his fortune.”

“You held his hand? He wouldn't have simply sat still, would he?”

“The moment my hand touched him, I could feel in his very hand that his whole body was boiling! What am I—a woman who spreads a bed for every man who flashes his teeth at me? Seeing the severity in my eyes, that Kakkai became subdued like a snake shut inside a box.”

“Oh! So you're a bold one! What happened next?”

“I spun him a tale that very soon he was going to become king of Mullaitheevu, and that after that even the kingdom of Kandy would fall at his feet!”

“And that fool believed it too?”

“When you're telling fortunes, whoever has come to have their fortune told, all you have to do is praise them to the skies, lift them up high, and tell them in the proper way that they are the one destined to rule this whole world! Even people who have no faith in fortune-telling or astrology will let their tongues hang out and drool as they imagine their future!”

“Is that so? Then I suppose the drool must be pouring from Kakkai Vanniyan's tongue right now!”

“Why just his tongue? It's pouring all the time like waterfalls from both corners of his mouth!”

<!-- source boundary: scan 274 → scan 275 -->

“What was the purpose of your going there? What secret did you learn?”

“I had heard that Lord North in Colombo and Kakkai Vanniyan are no longer on such close terms! I went there to find out whether it was true!”

“Did you find out?”

“How could I not?”

“How?”

“I told him, ‘You are the future ruler of Mullaitheevu! You will even be king of Kandy!’ The moment I poured all that out, he said, ‘How can that happen? I don't have that much military strength!’ So I threw in another line: ‘Don't be afraid! Lord North will not abandon a man he trusts!’”

“And he believed that too?”

“No! That was when he spat out the truth!”

“What truth?”

“Apparently Lord North doesn't even look at Kakkai Vanniyan these days! He has gone to Colombo seven times and lain in penance waiting for an audience, but never got one! You know Lord North's nature! As long as someone is useful to him, he'll lift him onto his head and dance around with him! Once he's no use, he's the curry leaf tossed outside the plate! That's the fate Kakkai Vanniyan has met!”

“For those who forget the welfare of their own people and debase themselves in the selfish hope that somehow they alone should prosper, this is the fate that follows. It cannot be avoided!”

“All right, all right! You've already gone off into philosophy! Listen to the cleverness I showed there!”

“Looking at you, you hardly seem like a woman suited to the work of a spy!”

<!-- source boundary: scan 275 → scan 276 -->

“Why? A sudden doubt?”

“A spy must not tell others about his own abilities! Everything must remain secret!”

“Do you think I don't know that art? I told you these secrets because you and I, Marthani and my elder brother Jeyaseelan are all engaged in the same work for the same purpose. If you like, just say that you're not that close to me—that you're not trustworthy—and I'll hide what ought to be hidden.”

“Don't be angry! I was only testing you, that's all!”

“Kakkai Vanniyan was worried that friendship would never again arise between him and Lord North, so I prescribed him a remedy. ‘Go to the Kannagiyamman temple and for three days, from midnight until dawn, stay completely alone and perform penance! You will receive her grace! Then Lord North will come running in search of you!’ That's the idea I gave him!”

“If he performs penance for three days and still doesn't regain North's friendship, will he continue to believe what you say?”

“What a complete madman you are! Why do you think I told Kakkai to go to the Kannagi temple? And to go alone?”

“Why?”

“Now you're asking the right question! He is a traitor to the Tamil people of Mullaitheevu. If he comes to the Kannagi temple with his troops, he'll escape. But if he comes alone, you can cut off his head and bring him down, can't you?”

“What?”

“Yes! That is how one of our enemies must be finished. Then betrayal will no longer cross our path! Don't hesitate! This is the king of Kandy's command!

<!-- source boundary: scan 276 → scan 277 -->

Kakkai Vanniyan must be finished first! After that, the army of the kingdom of Kandy and Pandaraka Vanniyan's army will be able to advance without obstruction to defeat and drive out the British!”

Thanigaimala sank into thought for a while. As his eyelids slowly closed, he felt as though four or five flower buds had fallen onto his shoulder! Without opening his eyes, he ran his fingers over them to see whether they truly were buds. Those buds were the beautiful fingers of Kala the Kurathi! Thanigaimala smiled gently. “Are you too a queen of chastity like Marthani?” he asked as he embraced her.

They were clearly enemies! Yet they themselves came forward and showered him with pleasure! Why should he say he did not want it? Why should he not enjoy it too and make defeating them his goal? Such was Thanigaimala's thought! Like removing one thorn with another, Thanigai's plan was to accept the pleasure Marthani and Kala had come to offer, give them pleasure in return—and in the end achieve victory! As one of the commanders loyal to his leader—as a bodyguard—as a true spy—he was steadfast; but in this matter he was simply an ordinary man.

“You cast aside Kakkai Vanniyan in that enormous fort, and yet you have this much desire for an ordinary commander like me?”

She gave no answer! She entwined herself around him! After all, she was Piyasili! For her softness, the embrace of rough men like Thanigaimala was an inexhaustible pleasure!

Pressed tightly against her, Thanigaimala swallowed and asked, “Kala! What do you want?”

<!-- source boundary: scan 277 → scan 278 -->

“I want every art, my king!” she said!

Commotion—then silence. Pretending to be startled, Kala cried anxiously, “I must go and see my brother and Marthani!”

He sent her upstairs. Exhausted, he lay down in his own room.

Kala and Marthani embraced one another.

“So, it was a success?” asked Marthani.

“Yes! Thanigai has agreed to go to the Kannagi temple and kill Kakkai Vanniyan!” said Piyasili, who was in the guise of Kala.

“Kakkai Vanniyan will be ready to finish this man off, won't he?” asked Marthani.

“All those arrangements are satisfactory!” said Kala.
