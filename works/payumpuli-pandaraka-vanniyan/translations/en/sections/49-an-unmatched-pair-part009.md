Since no beauty had arrived even by midnight for him to embrace and enjoy, Lord North drank far beyond measure and staggered about the room, scarcely knowing head from foot.

“My lord! Today we sifted through the whole of Colombo as though through a sieve, and at last we have brought this lamp of a household. Her husband has come along as her companion—but he is mute. He will roll about outside the room. We shall leave now,” the messengers reportedly said before withdrawing.

Already swaying in drunkenness, Lord North grew still more intoxicated by Marthani's ravishing beauty and became like a monkey let loose in a flower garden! She was a storm, yet she glided like a gentle breeze and brought him down through her daring skill. This queen of chastity, who had mastered the art of making him dance as she wished without allowing him even to touch her body, laid him out on that bed like a great python that had swallowed a cow!

Seeing that the moment had come, the mute Jeyaseelan opened all the secret chests of the women's quarters and seized many pieces of information that we needed. At the first glimmer of dawn, before day had properly broken, the two of them slipped away. Knowing that if they stayed any longer they might be caught by British soldiers, they came to Kandy and placed the secrets in my hands.

It is my wish that these skilled people should remain with you for at least a few days and assist you. Their own desire is to serve the kingdom of Kandy. At last they have accepted my request and are coming to you!

They are not only capable, but good people as well! When no woman wished to marry the cousin from her mother's side who had been born mute, this epic woman—

<!-- source boundary: scan 241 → scan 242 -->

Marthani—loved him without end and stood firm that she would marry him alone, and she fulfilled her vow. Many people stood in the way of that love-marriage. Facing them all down, Marthani accepted the mute man as her life companion!

I have sent such an admirable pair to you. I believe they are capable of adding still greater sharpness to the strategies you devise and to your methods of war, and of helping you win victory.

May God bring an end to our long separation and make us meet again soon!

Thus,

Your friend dearer than life,

Kannusami.

When Pandaraka Vanniyan finished reading the letter, rosewater-like drops glistened in his eyes, revealing his joy.

“Kuruvichchi! You too read this! This letter is not merely an example of the affection my friend bears me; it is also a fine testimonial showing how unmatched these two inseparable people are!”

Saying this, Pandarakan handed the letter to Kuruvichchi. She finished reading it and let out a long, deep sigh.

“Make arrangements for the two of them to stay,” Kuruvichchi ordered the spy Thanigai.

“I will meet you later. I have some important work,” said Pandarakan, taking leave of them and going into an inner room of the palace.

Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar did not fail to notice Jeyaseelan wink at Marthani with a victorious smile as he followed Thanigai.

<!-- source boundary: scan 242 → scan 243 -->

“Is your name Thanigai?” Marthani asked in a cool voice.

“Yes! Thanigaimala. They shorten it and call me Thanigai,” replied the spy as he led them away.

“Do you know how important the letter we brought is?” Marthani asked.

“I do not wish to know anything he has not told me,” said Thanigai.

With a smile lingering on his face, Jeyaseelan shook Thanigai by the shoulder and gestured as though to say, “Bravo! The right answer!”

“Why is your hand as soft as a flower?” Thanigai asked, touching Jeyaseelan's hand and gently stroking it.

For Piyasili, who yearned for men to touch her, her whole body was a flame at that moment!
