# Publisher's Note

Among Kalaignar's works, so many rare books continue to spread their fragrance forever in the garden of Tamil literature. Among them, the finest is _Sangathamizh_. We have the pride of having published _Sangathamizh_!

Now Rockfort is publishing Kalaignar's historical epic, _Payumpuli Pandaraka Vanniyan_!

The time and moment when we publish this historical epic are worth noting! Today, the book _Payumpuli Pandaraka Vanniyan_ will give Tamil soil the strength and encouragement it needs.

We express our thanks to Professor K. Anbazhagan, who provided the Foreword.

Our appreciation goes to artist Jayaraj, who drew the colour illustrations; artist-poet Amudhon, who designed the cover; and Subha Sundaram, who supplied the colour photograph of Kalaignar.

Chennai-32  
6-5-91

Rockfort Publications (P) Ltd.
