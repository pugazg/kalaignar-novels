# The Leader and the Lady!

The one who opened the door and came in was Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar! Thanigaimala, who had not expected her arrival in the least, started, rose to his feet, and asked in surprise, “You?”

“Yes, Thanigai! It is I! Do you understand why I have come?”

“I don't! I could never even have dreamed that you would come to my house! If you had sent for me, I would have come running!”

“Why? Is it beneath me to come here because this is your house? Am I to remain only in the palace of Mullaitheevu? When even cactus scrub seems to us, who stand in the field of struggle, like a lovely grove, what is lacking in this house where a good man like you lives? In what way is it lowly?”

“You are standing! Please sit down!”

Thanigaimala dusted off the seat and spread a cloth over it. Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar removed the cloth, set it aside, and sat directly on the seat. He kept looking at her eagerly, wondering what she was going to say.

“Do you know who the people who have come are?”

“Whom do you mean?”

“That mute man and his wife! I am asking about them!”

“The king of Kandy sent them, didn't he? That much is perfectly clear!”

“You are a spy! I am asking whether anything about them made you suspicious.”

“I was suspicious at first. Then, when I learned that the king of Kandy himself had sent a letter with them, I freed myself from that suspicion. Even so, like drizzle that lingers after the rain has stopped, a tiny drop of suspicion still remains in my mind!”

“It must not remain only a tiny drop, Thanigai! They say little drops make a great flood. A flood comes only when many little drops gather. So there is no use in your suspicion remaining a tiny drop. It must be a small spark. A spark can itself assume a gigantic form and grow until it can burn an entire town. In the same way, your suspicion must not dry up as a little drop; it must spread as a spark.”

“If you tell me clearly what you mean, I can act accordingly!”

“I too saw the king of Kandy's letter, didn't I? It was not written in his own hand—the signature alone was his!”

“Amma! The king of Kandy has sent letters like that before. They too bore only his signature. Someone else must have written the letter to his dictation!”

“What you say is right too! But one must look at everything together with its circumstances, place and time before arriving at a firm conclusion.”

“Is our leader suspicious too, as you are?”

<!-- source boundary: scan 251 → scan 252 -->

Thanigaimala! Don't you know our leader? The very embodiment of sacrifice! A fearless heart that longs to enter the field! A man who has resolved that he must live only for the Tamil people—and die for them too—but if enemies speak a little movingly and soften their voices, the trait of saying, ‘Ah, poor things!’ and believing them just as they are seems to have been born along with him!”

“Then say that he believes the king of Kandy's letter. And that he believes these people sent by the king of Kandy!”

“I am not refusing to believe them myself! Nor am I telling you to refuse! If something lying at your feet among vines and plants looks exactly like a green creeper, what is wrong in looking closely to make sure it is not perhaps a green snake? But he will not accept even a little of my thought! Nothing but sympathy for that mute man! Endless respect for that woman! ‘Ah! In what a terrible situation, right in the capital of Colombo, they showed their skill!’—he is lost in wonder!”

“Our leader is always ready to do anything for a person once he has trusted them. Don't I know how many people he trusted have deceived him? That accursed Kaakkai Vanniyan—there was even a time when he thought perhaps he could make a friend of him! I was the one who stood in the way and prevented such a danger! In his longing to see our heroic struggle succeed, he trusts people too easily!”

“He hates Kaakkai Vanniyan more bitterly than an *ettikkai* fruit. I understood that truth from my own affair—

<!-- source boundary: scan 252 → scan 253 -->

Good thing you warned him about Kaakkai beforehand!”

“Oh, heavens! Is he a human being? What runs through his body is not blood at all! They speak of a deadly serpent called Karkkodagan—the poison of that serpent is what runs through his whole body!”

“Thanigai! I came to speak to you about him too! I came on my own to talk about these people who claim to have been sent by the king of Kandy. But to speak to you about Kaakkai Vanniyan, he himself sent me to you!”

“What? Our leader?”

“Yes! It seems he feels somehow awkward speaking to you about it himself. That is why he sent me to talk to you!”

With a confused heart, yearning to understand some puzzle, Thanigaimala watched only the movement of Kuruvichchi's lips, waiting to hear what she would say.

“You know Pandarakan's sister Nalla Naachchiyar, don't you?”

“How could I not know even that? Isn't that Naachchiyar guarding the Trincomalee region? In the same way, another sister, Oomachchi Naachchiyar, is guarding the Panangamam region.”

“They are both guarding their regions very well. Nalla Naachchiyar guards Trincomalee skilfully—but our leader is troubled that she does not know how to guard herself properly!”

“What is this, Amma? It feels as though someone is dissolving tamarind in my stomach!”

“Tamarind is being dissolved in your stomach! But our leader is suffering over what happens when tamarind is dissolved in milk!”

<!-- source boundary: scan 253 → scan 254 -->

“We are all here precisely so that he need not suffer! Tell me everything!”

“Somehow that Kaakkai Vanniyan has deceived Nalla Naachchi and made her fall into a net of love—”

“What? What? Has Cupid's serpent swallowed a volcano? No, Amma! It cannot be! It simply cannot be! It is impossible!”

“I am telling you the truth! Kaakkai Vanniyan has deceived Nalla Naachchi by claiming that he is the son of a great merchant's household and that his name is Peermanandan. By someone's good fortune, that love has gone no farther than talk. Without knowing that the fraud is Kaakkai Vanniyan, Nalla Naachchiyar is in a hurry to marry him!”

“Shouldn't our leader simply tell Naachchiyar who he really is?”

“He knows how very tender his sister's heart is! He is confused, wondering what will happen to her when she hears such news, and what decision she might make.”

“Leave that responsibility to me. I will take care of it. Tell him not to worry. For people like us, your being by his side is itself a comfort!”

“All right! One of the two matters I came about is settled! The other one...”

“You already told me that at the very beginning! Suspicion must not be a tiny drop; it must be a spark! I will keep watch on this mute man and Marthani.”

Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar answered only with a smile at the corner of her lips, making it clear that she trusted Thanigaimala's skill and loyalty, and then she

<!-- source boundary: scan 254 → scan 255 -->

left. Thanigaimala followed her to the doorway to show respect and see her off. Kuruvichchi nodded farewell and disappeared from there.

Only then did Marthani, who had come downstairs carrying a plate of snacks, hide behind a partitioning barrier before either of them could see her and stand there out of sight.
