# Kandy Vikrama Raja Singan Memorial Muthu Mandapam

The grave of Kandy Vikrama Raja Singan—Pandaraka Vanniyan’s close companion and the last Tamil king to rule Lanka—had fallen into ruin on the bank of the Palar at Vellore. When Kalaignar returned to office, a beautiful memorial Muthu Mandapam was built at the site of the Kandy king’s grave, which had become overgrown with bushes, and it was opened on 1-7-90.
