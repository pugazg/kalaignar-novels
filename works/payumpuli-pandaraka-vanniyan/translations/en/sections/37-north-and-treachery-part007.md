—I had believed that you would protect the whole region of Kandy as well. You completely ignored what I, a loyal subject of the crown, had to say! You even came to think of me as an enemy! That is why I went to Colombo seeking peace of mind! Even after going there, I pleaded with Lord North only for the greatness of the kingdom of Kandy and for your rule here to continue. I have not taken part in anything against you. Nor will I. Do you know why? How can I forget that you are my daughter's husband? Can that affection simply disappear?”

Before Pilimathala had finished saying this, Governor North gave his bald head a tap and burst into laughter.

“Somehow you must get back to Kandy, without making it look as though you have turned against that Kannusami—and at the same time remain like a snake coiled around his leg! I am not going to proclaim Muthusami king of Kandy! If I made the proclamation, the people of Kandy would be angry with me, would they not? Muthusami himself will proclaim himself king of Kandy. If he does that, confusion will arise in the politics of the kingdom of Kandy, will it not?”

“Yes! There will be confusion! At the same time, the people of Kandy will think of it in another way too!”

“How will they think?”

“They will think that Muthusami has made such a proclamation only with Governor North's backing—and even so, they will be furious and say that they will not let him enter as king of Kandy. That is my view.”

“For a few days, confusion in Kandy's politics—then Muthusami enters Kandy with the military strength supplied by this North—the confusion grows a little more—and then war!

<!-- source boundary: scan 181 → scan 182 -->

Muthusami wins that war! Which means this North wins! Then British rule in Kandy! And Pilimathala as its agent!”

“The plan certainly sounds sweet! But I doubt whether it can succeed as long as Vikrama Rajasingan has Pandaraka Vanniyan's support!”

“What else can be done after Pilimathala has failed in the attempt to separate them? Do not build castles in the air imagining that the plan I am describing will be fulfilled tomorrow and that the day after tomorrow you can take up your post as our agent in the kingdom of Kandy! If the effort now under way in Tamil Nadu to subdue Kattabomman succeeds, large numbers of our troops there will be able to come to Lanka. That may take another six months, or even a year! Until then, we must patiently move our pieces. Once Kattabomman is subdued and our military strength there comes here, defeating Pandaraka Vanniyan—and defeating Vikrama Rajasingan—will become very easy for us! So without rushing, we must quietly weave our web of intrigue!”

“I am ready for anything!”

“Why would you not be? Are you not to be our agent in the kingdom of Kandy in the future?”

“Am I not that even now, my lord?”

“Good! You may return to Kandy soon! Within a day or two of your reaching Kandy, Muthusami's proclamation will appear! You must make a show of condemning that proclamation very harshly. You must continue to act the part of Kannusami's loyalist.”

While North and Pilimathala were speaking, a white officer strode up swiftly and stiffly, stood erect, saluted, and said the following to North.

<!-- source boundary: scan 182 → scan 183 -->

“Important news has arrived from Tamil Nadu! Thanapathi Pillai, who was like Kattabomman's shadow, has been captured at Kovilpatti—and has now been hanged at Nagalapuram! Our soldiers cut off his head, fixed it on a stake, and planted it opposite the Panchalankurichi fort!”

On hearing the news, Governor North at once drained half the cup of liquor in his hand, poured the rest over Pilimathala's bald head, and celebrated his delight.
