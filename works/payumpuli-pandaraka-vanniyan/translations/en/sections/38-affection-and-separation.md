# Affection and Separation!

“Not only did they hang Thanapathi Pillai and cut his body into pieces. The moment Ettappan heard that Kattabomman had agreed to go and meet that arrogant Englishman Bannerman, after listening to what he had said, he ran to Bannerman and urged him that this was the moment to attack the Panchalankurichi fort. Do you know what news reached Kattabomman while he was staying at Kolarpatti on the way? That Bannerman's forces had captured the Panchalankurichi fort! Within the next four days, Thanapathi Pillai too fell into the Company's hands and lost his life.”

Pandaraka Vanniyan told the king of Kandy this in a trembling voice, but with sparks of fire in his eyes. Deeply shaken, the king of Kandy said anxiously, “Is that true? Could such a thing have happened? It should never have happened!”

“I am only telling you the information that came from Tamil Nadu. Unable to return to the Panchalankurichi fort, Kattabomman was apparently surrounded by British troops at the Kolarpatti palace itself. He fought a great battle against those troops and finally escaped from them—

<!-- source boundary: scan 184 → scan 185 -->

They say the British Company's forces have been sent in many directions with Ettappan's help to capture him!”

Hearing Pandaraka Vanniyan's words, spoken from a heart that seethed with anguish, Vikrama Rajasingan prowled that hall like a tiger, as though such a calamity had befallen him personally. Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar, who had been listening to the two men, asked with feeling, “Is there no way to save the king of Panchalankurichi? Should we not think about that?”

“How can it be done in this situation? He and Vellaiya Thevan had often sent us word that we must remain cautious about the British. Now they themselves have been deceived by failing to remain cautious! I still have hope. Kattabomman will not fall into the Company's hands. Somehow he will increase his military strength, confront the British again, and retake the Panchalankurichi fort—that is what I expect.”

Kuruvichchi looked at Pandarakan, who spoke with hope, and said:

“Your hope is admirable enough! But after such a fall at Panchalankurichi, North and his official class here in Lanka will leap about with great enthusiasm! They will calculate that defeating the king of Kandy and you will be easy! Just as Kattabomman has an Ettappan in Tamil Nadu, here in Kandy the king's own father-in-law is present! Is that all? Kaakkai Vanniyan is waiting too! So we must think and act at once.”

<!-- source boundary: scan 185 → scan 186 -->

“What are you saying, Kuruvichchi? There is no use merely describing the danger! Should you not also tell us a way to escape it?”

“It has been many days since you left Mullaitheevu! While we are not there, could the British forces not devise a strategy to surround it?”

At that point the king of Kandy interrupted. “What if such a thing does happen? Would this Kandy palace refuse to give my friend Pandarakan a permanent place?” he said hastily.

Yes, he had indeed spoken in haste! He had spoken out of the rights of friendship! The change on Pandaraka Vanniyan's face showed that he did not accept those words.

“What is this, Kannusami? Why did you suddenly say such a thing? Though your words prove the depth of your friendship for me, where is even a little of the courage or statecraft required of one who rules a country?”

“If I have said anything that hurt you, forgive me, my friend!”

“It does not matter! Do not distress yourself over it! Nor can we dismiss what Kuruvichchi has said. I must leave for Mullaitheevu today itself and increase our military strength. You too must strengthen Kandy's army. North and his commanders will first set their sights on Mullaitheevu. They know that once I am finished, you alone will remain in the soil of Lanka crying out that British domination must not spread. Therefore, both of us must keep our forts, ramparts and warriors ready for a great war.”

<!-- source boundary: scan 186 → scan 187 -->

“Vairamuthu! Have I ever made it a habit to answer back to your word? Do what you think is right! From tomorrow itself, I will double the military strength of the Kandy kingdom as you say.”

“An increase in military strength alone will not be enough! We must also cut apart, with great skill, the poisonous web being spun by Pilimathala and Piyasili. That embodiment of betrayal who has disappeared from Kandy is bound to return somehow! Who knows what plan he will come with? In the same way, Piyasili, whom you have confined in the palace, will also be waiting for her moment.”

“Not only are you leaving me, you are also frightening me by naming those terrifying creatures!”

“I am not frightening you, Kannusami! I am only reminding you, that is all! My friend, what I am about to say will surprise you. It will also be bitter.”

“What are you going to say?”

“Thinking it to be statecraft, I agreed to your marrying Piyasili. I even encouraged you. But I never expected so many disasters to result from it. Somehow I temporarily blocked Pilimathala's intention and had you crowned king of Kandy. The daughter of the Madurai Palaiyakkarar, a princess, has been waiting for you. Her very name is Ilavarasi! By marrying her, you will gain not only a good wife, but also the great support of the Madurai Palaiyakkarar. With the wealth amassed by him, Kandy can be made prosperous. With the treasury you possess, you can increase Kandy's military strength against the British.”

<!-- source boundary: scan 187 → scan 188 -->

“What are you saying? Another marriage already?”

“What is going to happen is what deserves to be called a marriage! What happened before was no marriage! It was a stratagem against a deceit!”

“Would it not be proper for you to stay and conduct that marriage too before leaving?”

“Ah! Marriage—a woman—and a beautiful woman at that—is like divine nectar to you, is it not? Would you let it go? I have already informed the Madurai Palaiyakkarar of all the details. He sent word that the wedding should be held in Madurai. I did not agree. Soon the Palaiyakkarar will board a ship and come here with your future wife. Finish the wedding imagining that I am present to bless you!”

“Is that true? Will you not come?”

“I will come! I will come only after I make the British flee so thoroughly that they will never show their faces toward Mullaitheevu again—and after I hear that those scoundrels have been unable to enter Kandy either and have fled in confusion—then I will come to laugh and rejoice with you! Kannusami! You have grown accustomed to one kind of life. I, your friend Vairamuthu, have grown accustomed to another kind of life! But the aim of both of us is to destroy foreign domination!”

At that, the king of Kandy embraced Pandarakan and vowed, “We will surely triumph in our ideal!”

“What is it, Kuruvii? Does everything I say to my friend sound like mere dry talk to you?”

Kuruvichchi Naachchi smiled gently.

Thinking of how those two loving hearts had merged as one for the noble ideal they carried within, without worrying about bodily union,

<!-- source boundary: scan 188 → scan 189 -->

she breathed a sigh of happiness. That very day, Pandaraka Vanniyan left Kandy with Kuruvichchi Naachchi and his soldiers.

Just as he and his warriors crossed beyond the boundary of Kandy, in another region Pilimathala entered within Kandy's border.
