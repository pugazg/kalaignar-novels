# A Hundred Warriors Are Needed!

Thanigaimala rose the next morning utterly exhausted! The whole night had been spent in the intoxication of revelry stirred up competitively by Piyasili and Marthani. Having dozed off only a little toward dawn, he was suddenly jolted awake! He twisted and stretched his body to shake off his sluggishness, tried with great difficulty to suppress the yawn that kept forcing its way out despite him, failed, and hurriedly poured cold water over his head, bathed, dressed, and prepared to leave.

Though his body was weary from the night's pleasures, there was a thrill in his heart!

If Marthani gave him wine, Piyasili gave him her lips! If this one gave him her lips, the other swayed her waist! “One cheek for you, one cheek for me,” the two of them seemed to say as they ploughed his rough cheeks with their lips, sending him to the very topmost branch of pleasure!

Marthani was supposedly anguished that her mute husband had left her; Kala was supposedly distraught because she did not know where he had gone. Yet neither woman showed even the slightest sign of such worry as they savoured him bit by bit through the whole night. Thanigaimala could understand that much!

Just as Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar had suspected! They had indeed come to practise deceit! About that, he did not think there was room for two opinions—

and yet why should he be a fool who rejected the drops of honeyed pleasure that came pouring toward him of their own accord? So he enjoyed them to his heart's content! Coquetry, bawdiness—everything of the sort was second nature to those two women! Thinking he lay there deceived by them, they displayed every trick they had learned! To Thanigaimala, who pretended to sleep while fully awake, that night was indeed a night of pleasure!

“We must thank the king of Kandy a crore upon crore times! If he had not sent us here, would such good fortune ever have come our way?”

Saying this, Piyasili, in the guise of Kala, wrapped her arms around his neck and bit him playfully again and again!

“At first I truly was sad that my mute husband Jeyaseelan had run away! But now that this Thanigaimala has quenched all my thirst, I feel as though I could simply live with him forever!”

Saying this, Marthani took his arms and pressed them against her breast!

He enjoyed the drama of two demons assuming the forms of beautiful women and performing before him! And he enjoyed the women themselves completely!

With memories of that night washing over him, he left the room and climbed the stairs to see what was happening upstairs. Hearing the sound of his steps, Piyasili and Marthani, who had been awake until then, hurriedly lay down on the mats there and pretended to be sleeping deeply from exhaustion. When he reached the upper floor, he stood staring closely at them.

Apparently the two of them were muttering in their sleep! Yes—they were supposedly dreaming and muttering! And even that dream seemed to be one demonstrating how loyal both women were to the king of Kandy and to Pandaraka Vanniyan! That, apparently, was why they were muttering!

“No! No! I won't allow it!”

“I will not allow any danger to come to Pandaraka Vanniyan!”

“Long live the king of Kandy! Long live, long live Pandaraka Vanniyan!”

“Look! That traitorous fellow Kakkai Vanniyan is going to die at the Kannagi temple!”

Whether or not he understood that Piyasili and Marthani were deliberately muttering like this even in their sleep as proof of how loyal and virtuous they were, Thanigaimala cleared his throat loudly. Like people waking after a sound sleep, the two amorous women rubbed their eyes and, showing their artistry as though in their drowsiness they did not notice their clothes slipping and coming loose, asked in one voice, “You? Have you already got up and prepared to leave?”

“Yes! I have to search for Jeyaseelan. After that, I must go to the Vattrappalai Kannagiyamman temple,” said Thanigai!

“To the Kannagiyamman temple? Why?” asked Marthani, as though she knew nothing.

“Didn't I already tell you? Have you forgotten so soon? That traitor Kakkai Vanniyan will be there, having come alone for three days of penance! He will come by himself, and the two of us have made a plan to cut off his head...”

Before Piyasili could finish, Marthani covered her mouth and said, “Kala! Kala!! Now I remember! Enough—stop!” Then she moved close to Thanigaimala and thundered in a harsh voice, “Let even finding my husband be the next task! First get rid of that traitor and return!”

<!-- source boundary: scan 288 → scan 289 -->

“I'll come back tonight with his head! What reward do I get?”

Thanigai laughed as he prepared to go downstairs.

“What separate reward is left to give you? We have already given ourselves to you completely!” said Piyasili.

After they knew he had gone downstairs, Piyasili said to Marthani:

“He says he's going to bring back a head! Poor man—he may lose his own head and come back as nothing but a headless trunk!”

The two laughed at the thought of that fool. He, however, left the place as a man who understood everything, planning to make fools of those women, mounted his horse, and sped away.

“Thanigaimala wants to see you! He says it is very urgent!” Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar told Pandaraka Vanniyan, who was having a light meal. While the two were still talking, Thanigaimala came in.

“What is it? You said it was something urgent?”

“Yes! I urgently need some help!”

Without yet knowing what Thanigaimala was going to ask, Pandarakan and Kuruvichchi looked at one another.

“I serve you not only as a spy—I am also your bodyguard!”

“Yes. What of it now?”

“For a short time, I need to become commander of one of our units!”

<!-- source boundary: scan 289 → scan 290 -->

“I don't understand what you're asking, Thanigaimala!”

“I am asking only temporarily! You must send a hundred warriors with me!”

“A hundred warriors! With you?”

“Yes!”

“To fight whom? To capture which fort?”

“I am not one who speaks against your word! But one thing—I am now in a position where I cannot tell you any details. Please allow me to take a hundred warriors with me!”

“Kuruvichchi! Did you hear what Thanigai said? He wants warriors—but says he cannot explain why!”

It seemed Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar understood, without even having to scratch her head, at least part of the reason for Thanigaimala's request!

Since Kuruvichchi had warned Thanigai to be cautious of those who had arrived bearing a letter from the king of Kandy, she realized that it must be connected with that and that he was therefore asking for a hundred warriors as support.

“Do we need to consult astrology to place trust in Thanigai's word? If he says he urgently needs a hundred warriors, it must be for some important task. My view is that we should grant his request first and ask for the explanation afterward!”

“Why, Thanigaimala! Are any Company officers coming? Is it to finish them off?”

Pandarakan did not receive the answer he expected from Thanigaimala!

“Forgive me! I will never take a foolish path,” Thanigai said in a firm tone.

<!-- source boundary: scan 290 → scan 291 -->

“Hey, Thanigaimala! You will never take a foolish path? You floated all night in an ocean of bliss with two women who bewitch men—was that, then, a very intelligent path?”

No one asked him this question! It was Thanigaimala's own conscience that looked at him and asked it!

He answered even that to himself: “When ‘remove a thorn with another thorn’ is an old saying, why can't ‘conquer desire with desire’ be a new saying?” That was his answer!

“I trust Naachchiyar understands the importance of my task.”

“Okay! Should what Naachchiyar knows be something I am not allowed to know?”

Pandarakan was not angry; he asked this teasingly while looking at Kuruvichchi.

“Once you seize on something, you are stubborn!” Kuruvichchi said in a low voice, casting a sidelong glance at Pandarakan as she moved away.

“All right! Whatever the reason, it doesn't matter! Kuruvichchi! Grant Thanigaimala's request at once.”

As soon as Pandarakan said this, Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar signalled to Thanigaimala and led him away from there.

Even after they had gone far enough that Pandarakan could not hear them, Kuruvichchi asked Thanigai with the anxious feeling that it might somehow still reach his ears, “What are the hundred men for?”

He bent respectfully close to her ear and whispered something in secret.
