# Two Hearts

<!-- incoming audited continuation: scan 120 → scan 121; frozen Part004 English intentionally not imported into Part005 translation -->

Kaakkai Vanniyan, who had been making efforts with the plan of skilfully moving the *yaattuk kaayai*, opened his mouth to ask her something. Just then Kuruvichchi, seeing a few villagers step over the threshold to offer their condolences, turned the moment to her advantage. Folding her hands, she said, “Everyone is coming to offer condolences! Thank you very much!” Without actually telling Kaakkai Vanniyan to get up and leave, she made the meaning plain. Grinning, he saluted her and departed, staring at her as though he would devour her with his eyes.

Kuruvichchi let out a deep sigh with the feeling that he was finally gone. Even from horseback, Kaakkai kept admiring the beauty of her breast as it rose and fell with that sigh while he rode away. Such a scoundrel—even in a house of mourning!

Unable to recover from the anguish that Kuruvichchi had gone to that scoundrel's palace to rescue Sangili Thalapathi, Pandaraka Vanniyan entered his own palace.

The moment he stepped inside, he was told that a spy had arrived urgently from Kandy to see him.
