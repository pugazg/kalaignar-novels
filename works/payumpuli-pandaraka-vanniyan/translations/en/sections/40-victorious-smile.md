# Victorious Smile!

Not only where Piyasili's hand touched him, but wherever her eyes fell upon him, Vijayathunga thrilled. Wrapping the young woman's body in his gaze, he struggled to look away. He faltered.

Believing that his daughter would prove she could win, Pilimathala decided that this was the moment and left.

Vijayathunga knew very well that Pilimathala stood in the front rank among those mine-shaft men of selfishness who thought it did not matter what they lost so long as what they gained was office. Even so, was not the beautiful figure now standing before him the very image he had once wished to possess? For a little while, despite the eyes of his inner mind, Pilimathala vanished from his awareness. Only Piyasili filled his heart.

“What is it, Vijaya? Why are you staring as though you could swallow me whole and belch afterward?”

“Hmm! I am the parrot that waited for the silk-cotton fruit! I am watching the fruit after it has burst open.”

“Do you not know that this is a fruit longing for you to bite and eat it?”

“Then why did you refuse the marriage? You refused even to turn and look at me then!”

“What you said first was right! For me to be a peacock in your inner palace, the difference in status stood in the way! But have I ever refused to be the creeper in your embrace? You were the one who said you would touch me only after marriage! Was it not in anger at that that I walked away without even turning to look at you? Vijaya! Even when I went away like that, I knew you were standing behind me enjoying the sight of me.”

“No! Not at all!”

“Do not lie. The beauty of a woman seen from the front can conquer a man. But the beauty seen from behind can kill him!”

Still speaking, Piyasili took a cup of wine from the table there and turned to walk toward him to offer it. He watched. What she had said was true. He was dying indeed.

Keeping one cup in her own hand, she held another out to him. He hesitated. Before he could decide, the cup was already near his mouth. She herself gave it to him! He drank with delight! The great warrior even bared his teeth in a grin! That mistress of dalliance swayed her waist.

The body of the dead man now began to burn! He roasted in the fire kindled by that temptress!

“Enough wine, my dear. I want the nectar of your lips,” he said.

“I have no objection! But the door has not been bolted,” she said.

He bolted it! At once she embraced him!

In Pilimathala's office—the floor itself became a bed of soft cotton!

“My golden one! If only you had given yourself to me completely!”

“Only now do I understand how great a mistake I made then, Vijayathunga! What can I do? There is such a thing as fate written on my head—that I must tie myself to Kannusami and weep for the rest of my life!”

“Tch! Tch! It pains me that your youth should suffer like this!”

“But you are relieving that suffering now! I have become some man's wife and gone on with life. I have set myself the principle that it is enough if the thirst of my youth is quenched like this from time to time, and I live without crossing beyond that line!”

“A grotesque principle!
A vulgar explanation!
Is she a woman?
A crazed,
playful lunatic!”

That is how Vijayathunga ought to have muttered! But he did not mutter! As she kept speaking, he showered kisses from the crown of her head to the soles of her feet!

“May I come to your birthday celebration?”

“By all means! But you cannot give me a better gift at that celebration than what you are giving me now!”

“Why can I not?”

“Even if I am a prisoner confined in the palace, at the birthday celebration am I not the queen standing beside the king?”

“So?”

“If it is true that you are a brave man, let me see you kiss me on the cheek then!”

<!-- source boundary: scan 198 → scan 199 -->

“Would that not be a foolish thing to do?”

“Is what is happening now such a very wise thing?”

As Piyasili asked this, she held him tightly.

Once again he lost himself.

“My dear! For you I am ready to turn the very sky into a coloured cup and make the inexhaustible sea into wine to fill it!”

“When two bodies become one body, men naturally babble like this!”

“It is not babbling! It is a pledge! An oath!”

Delighted that she had now brought him completely under her control, Piyasili wet his whole face with the honey of her lips. He lay entranced. He lay there thinking all of heaven was paltry beside this.

“You need not turn the sky into a cup. You need not turn the sea into wine. I am not some mountain-sized woman who could drink all that! My desire is very ordinary. Fulfil just that one thing! Afterward I will always be your guava fruit! You will be the squirrel! What do you say, my dear? Is that all right?”

The moment she addressed him in that intimate way, the word itself seemed to cast a spell and began leaping and dancing brightly inside his heart.

“What is your desire, my golden one? Tell me right now and I will fulfil it, my treasure!”

He embraced her as he spoke. She, deftly combing through his hair with her fingers, said:

“Nothing! It is the same thing I asked you first!”

“What?” he asked. “How sweet it is even to hear you say it! My queen!”

<!-- source boundary: scan 199 → scan 200 -->

With her lips she pressed against his little moustache. Now he was ready to accept whatever command she gave.

“At tonight's birthday celebration, this queen will be beside the king of that hall! Then this king comes there! He gives me a bouquet as a birthday gift! I say thank you! Then... then...”

“I bow to His Majesty!”

“That is not it!”

“Then what?”

“The moment I say thank you, you embrace me and kiss me on the cheek!”

“I have given you so many kisses now... I will give you even more!”

“Not here! There!”

“What will the king think of me?”

“So many men! He will think you are just one among them! But one thing—if only you fulfil my wish today, from then on I will be queen to the king! But to you alone I will be a wellspring of the world of pleasure!”

She embraced him again. An unexpected feast for Vijayathunga! And with course after course! So he was well and truly caught.

“Very well! Your wish will be fulfilled!”

And the reward he received immediately for saying that—what a reward it was! It left him astonished, wondering how she had learned so many arts at such a young age.

The guarded door opened. One kiss! Both had a share in it! Taking leave, Vijayathunga walked away while still looking at her. She, meanwhile, gave refuge once again to the clothes that had lain like exiles and smiled a victorious smile.
