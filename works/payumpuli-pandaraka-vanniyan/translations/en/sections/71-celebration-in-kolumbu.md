# Celebration in Kolumbu!

In the capital city of Kolumbu, multicoloured fireworks were set off in front of Lord North's mansion, making the whole area look like broad daylight. Soldiers of the English Company, bottles of liquor in their hands, embraced one another; those who found the opportunity embraced white women, expressing their joy in drunken excitement. From time to time, the sound of exploding fireworks joined the roar of their victorious celebration. What was the reason for all that revelry, song, dancing, drink, women's dancing, and the shouts of intoxicated men?

The white crowd in Lanka had gone into a frenzy after hearing the victorious news that the lion who had roared that English domination must never set foot in Tamil Nadu—the ruler of Panchalankurichi, Veerapandiya Kattabomman—had been brought down. The rulers of domination believed that if this news spread across the island of Lanka, a land very close to Tamil Nadu where Tamil people lived and could be affected in one way or another by every event in Tamil Nadu, the force of opposition to the English among the Tamils of Lanka would weaken and they would shrink back in fear. That was why they danced at the death of a great warrior from the Tamil—

—land who had stood against them. They believed that Kattabomman's fall would reduce Pandaraka Vanniyan's fury in Lanka and that he would be forced to conclude that he should no longer make enemies of the English. That was why Lord North's mansion was celebrating on such a scale.

In the elegant hall of the upper mansion, Lord North had a woman lying across his lap on a silk mattress. With one hand he combed through her hair; with the other he emptied a cup of liquor.

She lay face-down, burying her face in his thighs and displaying the full beauty of her back. Not content merely to run his fingers through her hair, Lord North stroked her back and gently rubbed his feet against hers, warming his body.

“Hmm! Go on, tell me the rest! It grows more delicious the more you speak. Do you know how pleasurable it is for you to talk while lying in my lap and for me to listen while drinking?”

“Do you know how I feel? You should stop talking to me for a little while! I want to forget myself and lie silently like this, face-down in your lap!”

“Will you drink a little more?”

“Ayyo, no! It has already gone beyond the limit! I can see two of you now!”

“So you have that sort of desire too?”

“Chee! What are you saying? Just take it as though I said you have the strength of two men!”

“Whatever you say, I am very angry with you—

—you know?”

“Angry? Why?”

“What a magnificent feast! And such a feast of so many varieties! I too have enjoyed many women. But today's experience—ah! Are you not the one enjoying me, every last bit of me? You have delayed giving me this magnificent opportunity for so long. Should I not be angry about that?”

The graceful woman lying face-down gave a bubbling laugh. Her shapely body shook as she laughed—yes, like a veena slipped free of its cover and swaying up and down. With that shaking, the cup in Lord North's hand jerked sharply, and drops of liquor spilled from it onto the back of the intoxicating woman.

“Oh!” she cried in surprise. Before she could roll over and sit up, he wiped it away himself. Leaving her face-down position, she rolled onto her back, raised her head and smiled.

“How these eyes shine! How delicious these lips taste!”

He caressed her eyes with his fingers and planted a kiss on her lips.

“Dear North! Thank you!” she said, winding both arms around his neck.

“There is still so much I must give you, and so much you must give me! Are we already at the chapter of thanksgiving?”

Lord North gathered her into his arms with a drunken laugh.

“North!” she moaned with pleasure.

“Piyaseeli!” he murmured thickly with desire.

The nearby lamp was dimmed further and moved away. The wine cup fell from the bed, spilled the liquor remaining in it onto the floor, rolled away and stopped in a corner.

The lamp grew bright again. She rose and stood naked as at birth, pouring liquor from the wine vessel into two cups. With the weariness of pleasure upon him, Lord North embraced her and offered her the liquor in his hand; she pressed the drink in her own hand to his lips.

They reclined on the bed again.

“Piyaseeli! How did you escape from Pandarakan's strict guard? Tell me!”

Piyaseeli laughed and casually threw one leg across Lord North.

“What are you laughing at?” he asked, entwining himself with her.

“Nothing! When I think of how I escaped, even I find it funny!” Piyaseeli said.

“What extraordinary comedy was that? Should I not know it and enjoy it too?”

North held Piyaseeli tightly.

“Do you know how I escaped?”

“Hmm! Tell me! Here is an advance payment for telling it!”

He caught Piyaseeli's lips as though seizing them, kissing them until they were blood-red. Even if an insatiable glutton were given a thousand such kisses, would she ever say it was enough?

“Hmm! Tell me! How did you escape?”

“I will tell you!”
