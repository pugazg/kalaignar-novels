When foes behold  
King Pandara Vanni's army,  
they flee and hide, wondering:  
“Does life still remain in the body, or has it gone?”

Even when spoken, words fall short  
of the valour of that Tamil elder;  
there are, too, the heads of white lords  
that he hacked to pieces!

—Ezhuchchi Kavignar Kasi Anandan
