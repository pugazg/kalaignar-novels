# The Heaven She Found!

With Nalla Naachchiyar's heart burning like fire and her body burned as well, she raged with the fury of doomsday. Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar stood mute and bewildered for a moment, not knowing what to say to her.

“I have betrayed our land! I have betrayed our people! I have betrayed my brother! Why, I have betrayed even myself!”

Her shattered heart wailed like a solitary lament in a cremation ground.

“Nalla Naachchi! You have betrayed no one! You are incapable of betrayal! Would a mother's milk kill her child? In the same way, the poisonous fang called betrayal could never sprout in your pure heart!”

As she answered, Kuruvichchi moved anxiously toward Nalla Naachchi.

But Nalla Naachchi would not let her come near.

“Please do not come near me! I am a sinful creature! I stand here as a disgrace to our very people because I could not tell the difference between a snake and a green vine! If you can do me one favour now—

<!-- source boundary: scan 342 → scan 343 -->

—perhaps, if there is such a thing as another birth and I happen to be born a woman again, that favour will make me remember you again and again and thank you! Will you do that favour?” cried the woman whose body was still scorched.

“I will do whatever favour you ask! First let me come near you! I will put out the fire. Then ask for whatever you need!” Kuruvichchi said, taking two steps toward her.

Nalla Naachchi moved two steps backward.

“The favour I need is to die pierced by the sword in your hand! Give me that sweet death! I must not live any longer! I cannot live! I do not wish to live!”

“What? You want to die by my hand? Is it to commit that sin that I came running here?”

“Yes! I must die by your hand! You have accepted my brother as your leader—a man who has dedicated himself to the land and our people and walks upon burning embers for them. You have laid every pleasure and comfort at his feet, taken a severe vow like an ascetic, regarded him as your very shadow, and live only for him. There is no other atonement for me—for my sinful deed—greater than dying by the hand of a pure woman like you!”

“Listen to what I am saying, Nalla Naachchi! Like storms forming over the sea, however many troubles and problems may have to be faced, our leader Pandarakan gives them priority and confronts them. But do not forget that he has set aside a special place in his heart for both of you, his beloved sisters!

<!-- source boundary: scan 343 → scan 344 -->

“That is precisely why I am tormented! That is why I am desperate for my life to be taken at once! With such trust he appointed me to Thirigona Malai and entrusted me with responsibility for guarding its rule! Did I discharge that responsibility properly? No! In the name of love, have I not betrayed our very people? I do not want to be alive when my brother sees me! If he sheds even a single tear for me, it will scorch this traitor's heart. Ayyo, I cannot bear that! Before then I must find peace in the lap of death! Please grant me that alms with the sword in your hand!”

“I cannot! I absolutely cannot! All of us must remain alive and protect our leader! If he is protected, our people, our soil and our honour will all be protected,” Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar said firmly.

“What did you say? You cannot? You and I must protect Pandaraka Vanniyan? For what? Why? Kuruvichchi! Do you think I do not know you? Do you think I do not understand why you go about with Pandarakan? He believes that you hold him dearer than life itself! But am I not the one who knows who you really are?”

Kuruvichchi stood like a tree as words she had never expected poured in volleys from Nalla Naachchi. Nalla Naachchi continued.

“My brother Pandarakan has a country! The people of that country trust him! He has a fort and battlements, and his flag flies there! When a powerful man with wealth and influence lies infatuated with you, would you simply let him go? Would you not bend him to your will and make him your slave?”

Kuruvichchi cried out without realizing it. “Nalla Naachchi—

<!-- source boundary: scan 344 → scan 345 -->

—what are you saying?” Kuruvichchi's heart sounded like the fiery breath of a furnace.

“I am speaking only the truth about you! Swear that you love my brother purely! You cannot say it! You go about pretending that you shower affection on my brother! He is a fool! A man who believes everything white is milk! All your claims that you are protecting him are empty show! Words of pretence! I know this very well!”

“Nalla Naachchi!”

Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar could only cry out her name. But contained within that one word were the questions: “You sinner! Is this how you speak to me? Do you say this about me, when I believe that my body, my possessions and my very life are all Pandarakan?”

As though she understood those questions, Nalla Naachchiyar deliberately tried to anger Kuruvichchi still further.

“You are ruin walking on two feet! My brother does not know that this beautiful enchantress is a seducer! Because he does not know the stories of countless men swimming in your sexual revelries, he foolishly believes you are a spotless maiden, a purified holy woman, a jasmine-vine who has renounced everything and lives only for him—and he is ruining himself!”

Before Nalla Naachchiyar had even finished speaking, Kuruvichchi Naachchiyar, having completely lost patience, sprang toward her with her sword, crying, “What did you say?”

That was exactly what Nalla Naachchiyar had expected. That was why she had woven and spread a net of scorching, harsh words. Kuruvichchi had fallen into that net and raised her sword—

<!-- source boundary: scan 345 → scan 346 -->

—while Nalla Naachchi opposed her with another sword. The two blades began to clash. Kuruvichchi struck aside Nalla Naachchi's sword as it was raised swiftly over her head, and the blade flew away somewhere. It had not fallen because of the force of the attack—Nalla Naachchi herself had deliberately let it slip. With the same momentum with which she had knocked that sword away, Kuruvichchi's blade came down and stood pointing straight ahead. Seizing that instant, Nalla Naachchiyar rushed forward and drove her own body onto the sword. A fountain of blood burst from the body that had already been scorched by fire. Suddenly Nalla Naachchi's face blossomed—with joy. Kuruvichchi understood nothing. She swept Nalla Naachchi into her arms and lifted her.

“Forgive me! I knew you would not strike me dead, and that is why I abused you so wickedly! Forgive me for that! This traitor is dying by the hand of a pure woman! This itself is heaven for me!”

A waterfall of tears surged from Nalla Naachchi's eyes. With her eyes alone she begged Kuruvichchi's forgiveness. Kuruvichchi was overwhelmed with anguish at the thought that, in her haste, she had been deceived.

Pandarakan arrived there with his division of troops. All he could see was Nalla Naachchiyar lying dead in Kuruvichchi's lap.

In a trembling voice, yet very quietly, he said, “Kuruvichchi!” as he bent down and looked at his sister.

“Your sister sought atonement for her mistake by my hand,” Kuruvichchi said through tears. Pandaraka Vanniyan stood shaken, lifted his beloved sister in both arms, and stared wide-eyed at Kakkai Vanniyan's fort before him, where the flames were dying down—
