# Today or Tomorrow? Why?

“Come on, tell me in a little more detail! How did you manage to complete those arrangements?”

Marthani kept looking eagerly at Piyasili.

“Marthani! Do you think I am not going to tell you? Of course I am. First you tell me—how did you make Thanigaimala believe the drama that the mute Jeyaseelan had run away?”

“You and I have both learned the art of making a man fall into the net, haven't we? The curves and bends nature has given our bodies—and, more than that, the false, counterfeit love that flows from our hearts, frothing and overflowing like foam surging in a liquor jar—wouldn't all that lay not just this one Thanigaimala, but the whole race of men, at our feet? When it comes to the drama of bewitching great warriors, you are more skilled than I am!”

Hearing this praise, Piyasili smiled with pride. Even so, remembering that she had failed to bring Pandaraka Vanniyan under her sway, she heaved a deep sigh.

“I could not conquer Pandarakan with my beauty—with my grace—with seductive words—with the sweep of lustful eyes! But have I at least managed to kill him? Until one of those two things happens, I will have no peace, Marthani!”

“I understand you! Even if you are going to kill him—

before that, only if you embrace him, enjoy him, and satisfy your desire will your soul find peace even after you grow old and die!”

“When God has granted me the boon of finding pleasure with ever-new men, why does Pandarakan alone refuse even to bare his teeth at me, Marthani?”

“What can we do? There are fools like that too! But I must say, this Thanigaimala—if we leap eight feet, he leaps sixteen!”

“Marthani! Did he leap only sixteen feet with you? With me he leapt thirty-two! He must have been terribly starved!”

“Yes! A man who had been ceaselessly sniffing out enemies drowned completely in the intoxication of desire! That is why, when I cried and wailed and made a great scene saying that you were missing—that is, that my beloved husband Jeyaseelan was missing—the fool believed it completely! He set off saying, ‘I will go right now, find him and bring him back!’ Even then I did not let him go without more—a whine! An embrace! A kiss! Poor man, his head was spinning.”

“Yes—he did not even come and tell you that he could not find him! Kala, he did not tell me either that he could not find your brother. Why?”

“By then he had already seen the very enchantment of the world sitting in his room in your form! He must have forgotten everything because of that!”

“I even have a fear that Thanigaimala may have understood who we are and may be deceiving us!”

“Has even Piyasili, that treasury of experience, still not understood the weakness of these men? Mere rosy skin turns some men into flies caught in a spider's web! Are you and I only red roses? I myself lose my heart seeing your waist bend like the neck of a peacock with mottled plumage! What need is there even to ask about Thanigaimala? The man who tasted me as though tasting fruit must have tasted you as though tasting fruit juice! Believe me for certain! From now on Thanigaimala will be as tame before us as a snake shut inside a box! But leave that aside—you said all the arrangements were satisfactory, yet you never told me how!”

“I wrote a letter and left it behind, then set out from here and went straight to Kakkai Vanniyan's fort. At first the fort guards refused to let me in. Then I gave the guard my father Pilimathala's signet ring and the letter he had given for Kakkai and had them sent inside. Kakkai Vanniyan immediately ordered that I be brought in. I went inside. There he was, emptying a liquor jar.”

“You must have been delighted at the sight!”

“He was the one who was startled first by my male disguise! So that I would not deepen his suspicions, I threw off the disguise in an instant and stood before him. ‘Piyasili!’ he shouted! A wonderfully fine bear-like voice! I was hungry too!”

Laughing, Marthani said, “That was an elephant's hunger!”

“Just because it is popped sorghum, can one let it go?” came Piyasili's reply!

“All right, all right! Tell me what happened next!”

“What always happens in my life! I tried to move closer and sit beside him! He seized me in a monitor-lizard grip—

<!-- source boundary: scan 282 → scan 283 -->

and held on! ‘Do not let go, my king,’ I said! One, two, three—the liquor jars there were all emptied! I decided that the work I had come for could wait, and finished my own business first. It was not complete satisfaction. Still, not bad. The consolation was that Thanigaimala was there for the next stage.”

“Piyasili! Did you explain the purpose of our mission? Did he agree to it?”

“He praised Pilimathala's plan as magnificent. Apparently he is maintaining secret contact with the British Company in Colombo and frequently sending them news from Mullaitheevu! There was one thing I liked very much about Kakkai Vanniyan!”

“You are going back to the bedroom again—come to the point!”

“I am telling you the point! No one has yet been born who can surpass him in the art of treachery! He said his ambition was to enslave Mullaitheevu to the British, have Pandaraka Vanniyan captured as a British prisoner, and become Lord North's trusted friend—and that this was the very purpose for which he had been born. When he said that, I embraced him just as he was and gave him twenty kisses!”

“Did he say nothing about the kingdom of Kandy?”

“After receiving twenty, would he remain silent? ‘This Kakkai for Mullaitheevu! That eagle for Kandy! These two alone will be kings from now on,’ he said!”

“An eagle? Who is that?”

“My father Pilimathala—that is how beautifully he refers to him.”

“Very good! Hmm! Go on!”

“Pandaraka Vanniyan's strength must be reduced step by step. The first target is to finish off Thanigaimala. We are to say that Kakkai Vanniyan will come alone to the Vattrappalai Kannagi temple for three days of penance, and make Thanigaimala go there alone! Then finish Thanigaimala at the Kannagi temple! Kakkai welcomed and accepted this plan.”

“What next?”

“Pandarakan's sister Nalla Naachchiyar is apparently in love with him without knowing that he is Kakkai Vanniyan!”

“Is that so?”

“Yes! Kakkai is apparently posing as a great merchant and acting as Nalla Naachchi's lover!”

“Then we can use that love affair itself to create hostility between Nalla Naachchi and Pandarakan!”

“Yes—there is a plan for that too! Once hostility arises between Nalla Naachchiyar and Pandarakan, Pandaraka Vanniyan's influence over Thirukonamalai will disappear. By bringing Nalla Naachchi under our control, we can cut Pandarakan's strength in half. After that comes Oomaichchi Naachchi in Panangamam—Pandarakan's other younger sister. Destroy her—or turn her too into Pandarakan's enemy. In this way, after cutting off Pandaraka Vanniyan's powerful wings and leaving him unable to fly, we can lock him in the Parangis' prison!”

“Bravo, Piyasili! Bravo! If Kakkai Vanniyan is going to arrive at the Vattrappalai Kannagi temple tonight with the proper arrangements—

<!-- source boundary: scan 284 → scan 285 -->

then why not send Thanigaimala there today itself and finish the matter successfully?”

“No! We can send Thanigai tomorrow!”

“Why delay by a day?”

“I feel like spending this one night with him!”

“What, Piyasili—is that what matters now? Shouldn't we pay attention to the work we came to do?”

“Marthani! We can divide this one night with him between us—half for you, half for me. All right?”

“All right! Do as you wish!” Marthani said, pretending as though she disliked the idea, and finally agreed.
