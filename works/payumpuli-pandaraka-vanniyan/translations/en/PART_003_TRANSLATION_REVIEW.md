## 3. Source-alignment corrections discovered during editorial review

Two English references were corrected because the verified Tamil made the intended agency explicit.

### E10 / scan61

Source-checked wording:
- `He will be eager to know whether I met you...`

Maintained wording:
- `Kattabomman will be eager to know whether I met you...`

Reason:
- Tamil explicitly names `கட்டபொம்மன்`; the former English pronoun could incorrectly point back to Vellaiyathevan.

### E12 / scan73

Source-checked wording:
- `Kuruvichchi Naachchi's unwavering love for Pandaraka Vanniyan, who had led the festival...`

Maintained wording:
- `the unwavering love that Kuruvichchi Naachchi, who had led the festival, bore Pandaraka Vanniyan...`

Reason:
- Tamil attaches the festival leadership to **Kuruvichchi Naachchi**, not Pandaraka Vanniyan.

These are English-only source-alignment corrections. Canonical and assembled Tamil remain unchanged.
