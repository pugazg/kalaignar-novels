## Register policy

Krishnayyar's marked colloquial/Brahmin-Tamil speech will be represented through informal English rhythm, contractions and syntax where appropriate. **No invented English ethnic accent** is permitted.

## Source-oddity policy

Protected Tamil stays protected. If fluent English necessarily hides an oddity, the Tamil source form and the translation decision must be recorded here or in the batch review; canonical Tamil must not be changed.
