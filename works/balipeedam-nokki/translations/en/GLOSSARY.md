# English Translation Glossary — பலிபீடம் நோக்கி

This glossary records translation choices locked after the Batch 1 pilot review. It is a translation-consistency aid; it does **not** alter or normalize the canonical Tamil `pages/` layer.

## Locked core terms

| Tamil source | English policy | Pilot decision |
|---|---|---|
| `பலிபீடம்` | **sacrificial altar** | governing metaphor; do not alternate casually with shrine/platform/scaffold |
| `பலிபீடம் நோக்கி` | **Towards the Sacrificial Altar** | working English title |
| `பலிபீடம் நோக்குங்கள்` | **turn / look towards the sacrificial altar** according to immediate syntax | retain the directional force of `நோக்கு`; do not mechanically make every occurrence “go to” |
| `பலிபீடம் நோக்கிகளே` | **O you bound for / facing the sacrificial altar** | source coins an address; preserve its rhetorical strangeness |
| `ஆரியம்` | **Aryanism** when functioning as an ideological/social force; **Aryan** adjectivally | do not recast as modern racial science |
| `ஆரிய பலிபீடம்` | **Aryan sacrificial altar** | preserve polemical metaphor |
| `தன்மானம்` | **self-respect** in movement/political contexts; **dignity** only where sentence sense requires | pilot locks `self-respect` for the ideological usage |
| `மறம்` | **valour** / **martial valour** | preserve martial force |
| `கொற்றம்` | **triumph** | used with `மறம்` in the pilot rhetorical pair |
| `பகுத்தறிவு` | **rationalism** / **reason** according to syntax | later conclusion must retain movement-era force |
| `யாகசாலை` | **sacrificial hall** | avoid generic “ritual hall” where the sacrificial frame matters |
| `அஸ்திரம்` | **weapon** | may retain `astra` only if a later passage makes the Sanskritic register materially important |
| `வேதியர்` | **Vedic priest** where the source uses it as a character/social designation | do not silently substitute a different caste title |

## Pilot source oddities

### `குதுமன்றி`

The audited source confirms the unusual form `குதுமன்றி` in:

`சோறும், குதுமன்றி எதுவுமறியா ஒரு சோதா...`

The pilot translation renders the phrase contextually as **“a weakling who knew nothing beyond food and the tuft”** while explicitly recording the exact source form here. Do not alter the Tamil source to a more familiar spelling.

### `விருதா`

The source prints the quoted form `‘விருதா’` in:

`வீரத்தை விலை கேட்கும் ஒரு ‘விருதா’...`

Because the lexical force is source-specific and not safely recoverable merely from grammar, the English pilot retains **`virudha`** while translating the surrounding phrase. Do not silently replace it with an inferred insult.

### `மஹா கனம்`

Retain as **“Maha Ganam”** when directly echoing the source's quoted label. Do not supply an expanded historical office/title unless a separately marked editorial note is later justified.

### `அன்பு(?)`

Preserve the source's parenthesized question mark: **loving(?)**. The punctuation is rhetorical evidence, not noise.

## Rhetoric and form

- Repeated questions remain repeated; do not compress them for English style.
- Exclamation clusters and ellipses may be lightly regularized only enough for legibility; their rhetorical escalation must remain visible.
- Film sound effects are transliterated rather than replaced with invented English onomatopoeia when the source sound itself is part of the texture (`குபீர் குபீர்`, `கட கடா`, `குடு குடு`).
- The internal `ராயசம் வெங்கண்ணு — தஞ்சை சரித்திரக் கதை` title card is always presented as an **internal sequence** of the one work.

## Political / religious language

The translation reports Kalaignar's historical rhetoric as written. It does not soften attacks on Aryanism, caste hierarchy, Brahminical structures, Hindu religious institutions, or social practices, and it does not add present-day endorsement, condemnation, or explanatory claims to the translated prose.
