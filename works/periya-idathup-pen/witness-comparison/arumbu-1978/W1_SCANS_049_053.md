## Comparison normalization

The comparison records source-visible textual differences.

Not counted as variants by themselves:

- ordinary line wrapping;
- physical-page repagination;
- whitespace around punctuation;
- typographic quote-glyph shape;
- hyphen/em-dash glyph style when punctuation function is unchanged.

Counted and recorded:

- lexical wording;
- source-visible word segmentation / sandhi differences;
- inflectional differences;
- punctuation mark changes that alter source punctuation;
- headings / separators;
- additions / omissions;
- non-textual illustration differences separately.
