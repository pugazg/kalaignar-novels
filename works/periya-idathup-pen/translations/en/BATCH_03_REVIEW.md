## Voice / rhetoric review

Kannamma's testimony is deliberately self-justifying and internally contradictory. The English preserves that viewpoint rather than reconciling it with Uthandi, Kumudha, Veeran or the later Kannamma conclusion.

Reviewed decisions:

- `விபசாரி` → **adulteress** in Kannamma's self-description, consistent with the immediate extramarital-sex frame;
- `பக்திமணி` / `பக்த சிரோன்மணி` → **jewel of devotion / crown-jewel among devotees**, preserving devotional elevation and irony;
- `வைதீக உலகம்` → **Vaidika world**, retaining the source's polemical register without adding outside religious history;
- `வாராகாவதாரம் / பூமா தேவி` → **Varaha incarnation / Bhuma Devi**, translating only the allusion present in the source;
- `வேசி` is rendered **whore** when it appears as a harsher imagined insult than `விபசாரி`;
- Veeran/Ulaganathar comparisons retain their exaggerated ridicule rather than being neutralised;
- pregnancy termination retains Kannamma's own violent vocabulary (`கொலை`, `யம லோகம்`) without converting the passage into external medical commentary;
- narrator claims that divine forgiveness excuses continued misconduct are preserved as Kannamma's rhetoric, not endorsed as editorial fact.

Result: **PASS**.
