1. `என் உப்பைத்தின்றவன்` — **“the man who ate my salt”**. The service/patronage idiom is preserved rather than replaced with a modern explanatory paraphrase.

5. `பத்திர காளி` — retained as **“Bhadrakali!”** in Ulaganathar's invective. No outside theological explanation is inserted into the prose.
