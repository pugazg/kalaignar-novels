## Source-sensitive English decisions

Three points received explicit pilot review:

1. `சாகும்போது என்ன சங்கார வேண்டியிருக்கு!` remains unusual in the audited Tamil. The pilot uses **“Why all this ceremony when we're going to die!”** as a cautious contextual rendering. No Tamil normalization was made.
2. `வீரனத்திக்கு முக்காட வைத்தன` is rendered **“put a veil over Veeran's daring”**, preserving the printed metaphor rather than silently correcting the source form.
3. The initial draft sense for `அந்த மகானுபாவன் மாதிரி பார்க்கக்கூட இல்லை` was reconsidered during source review. In the surrounding passage Ulaganathar is being mocked for his appearance immediately before his delusion about his own beauty; the reviewed English therefore reads **“this great soul was not even much to look at.”** This is a translation-side correction only. Canonical Tamil remains unchanged.

The ritual phrase `பொங்கல் பள்ளயம் பாவாடை` is retained as **“pongal, pallayam, pavadai”** rather than expanded from unsupported outside assumptions.
