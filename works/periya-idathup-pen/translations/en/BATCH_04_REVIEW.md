Kumudha's letter is translated as her own moral and emotional testimony, not corrected by later narrators.

Reviewed decisions:

- `அத்தான்` remains **Aththan** on important uses, following the established kinship policy;
- `ருசுவாகி` is rendered as **establish / prove** Kumudha's innocence rather than given a legal precision not supplied by the source;
- `படிந்த மனப்பான்மை` is rendered as **deeply ingrained ways**, preserving her self-description without importing a modern ideological label;
- `வெள்ளாட்டியாக்க` is rendered **make me a sacrificial goat**, supported by the immediately following goat-before-slaughter image;
- the closed-room sequence is translated directly from Kumudha's experience; later narration is not used to rewrite what she says here;

- Sita/Ravana, Nalayini and Kailasa references are translated only to the extent present in the Tamil.
