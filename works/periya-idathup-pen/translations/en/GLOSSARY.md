# English Translation Glossary — பெரிய இடத்துப் பெண்

This glossary records recurring English decisions for the derived translation layer. It does **not** authorize any change to canonical Tamil. If a translation problem raises doubt about Tamil wording, return to the canonical page/source audit first.

## Pilot source-sensitive readings

These are English-side decisions only; exact Tamil remains preserved in canonical records.

- `இதயப் படுதா` — rendered **“the curtain of his heart”** to preserve the source's figurative surface rather than replace it with a normalized Tamil assumption.
- `சங்கார` in `சாகும்போது என்ன சங்கார வேண்டியிருக்கு!` — pilot renders **“Why all this ceremony when we're going to die!”** cautiously from the local rhetorical context. The exact unusual Tamil form remains authoritative; this English may be revisited in final review if stronger source evidence emerges.
- `விவகார தாட்சண்யமாக` — rendered **“an affair of discretion”** in the local liaison/fear-of-exposure context.
- `வீரனத்திக்கு முக்காட வைத்தன` — rendered **“put a veil over Veeran's daring”** to preserve the printed metaphor without silently normalising the Tamil form.
- `மாதிரி பார்க்கக்கூட இல்லை` — after pilot source review, rendered **“was not even much to look at”** because the immediate passage contrasts Ulaganathar's appearance with his mistaken belief that Kannamma adored his beauty. This was an English interpretation correction only; canonical Tamil was unchanged.
- `கொவ்வை இதழ்கள்` — rendered **“kovvai-fruit lips”** rather than replacing the source image with a generic “red lips.”

## Pilot style lock

After Batch 1 review:

- source satire and abrupt exclamations remain visible in English;
- unusual source metaphors are not silently domesticated;
- source dots/ellipses are retained where they carry voice or an apparent textual gap;
- one Tamil paragraph normally maps to one English paragraph;
- canonical page joins remain traceable through HTML comments;
- no narrator is corrected using information revealed by a later narrator.

Add new glossary entries only when a recurring translation decision is actually encountered and source-supported.
