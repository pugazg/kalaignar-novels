## 2026-09-03 Part-005 source-resolution update

Five previously qualified tape-obstructed scans, **215–219**, were directly reviewed by the user against the attached source and are now closed.

### Scan 215 corrections

- `பாமளாவும்` → `பரிமளாவும்`
- `கொழுந்தே` → `கொழுந்தாம்`
- `என்று தழுதழுத்த குரலிலே கேட்டான்` → `என்று தழுதழுத்த குரலிலே அவன் கேட்டான்`

### Scan 216 corrections

- `புண்ணுக்கு மருந்து கேட்கிறாய்.` → `புண்ணுக்கு மருந்து கேள்; தருகிறேன்.`
- `அவன் வீறுகொண்டு` → `அவன் வீரனாக`

Scans 217/219 were confirmed without lexical correction; duplicate scan 218 was synchronized to corrected printed page 214.

The correction was propagated through canonical Tamil, assembled Chapter 24, English Chapter 24, Part-005 audit/review, whole-work bilingual review and release status.

## English correction impact

The release English now reads:

- **Parimala** rather than the earlier **Pamala** at scan 215;
- **“I am the father who begot you, the shoot of my clan—”** for corrected `கொழுந்தாம்`;
- **“Durai! Ask for medicine for the wound; I'll give it. Do not ask me to pierce it with a spear. I cannot!”**;
- **“But he did not stand as a warrior and fight.”**

The scan-215 addition of Tamil `அவன்` before `கேட்டான்` does not require a separate English wording change because the translation already explicitly says “he asked”.
