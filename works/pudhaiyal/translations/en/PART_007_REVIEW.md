## Bilingual-review corrections made

The bilingual pass found and corrected the following English-layer issues. **No canonical Tamil text was changed.**

1. **Chapter 36 — Jahangir title overreach**  
   The draft had expanded Tamil `காவலன் ஜஹாங்கீர்` to **“Emperor Jahangir.”** The bilingual review removed that externally stronger historical title and now keeps the source-bound wording as **“the ruler Jahangir”** in the bedchamber passage.

2. **Chapter 38 — `பத்தாயிரம் முத்தாரம்`**  
   The draft had over-interpreted this as **“ten thousand strings of kisses.”** The review restored the conservative source-facing image as **“a garland of ten thousand pearls”**, avoiding an invented hidden pun while retaining the romantic contrast of the passage.

3. **Chapter 39 — omitted `முகத்தில்`**  
   The source-check draft correctly established that the torn cloth/skirt is requested for **Saibu**, but had dropped the explicit `முகத்தில்` detail. The reviewed English now tells the Maharaja to toss the torn cloth/skirt **in Saibu's face**, preserving the source's comic physical instruction.

These corrected spans were rechecked against the controlling Tamil.

The earlier controlled English source check had already corrected two additional English-only issues without touching Tamil: Chapter 38 `இந்த வீடுதான்!` is represented as **“Of this house!”**, and the Chapter-39 alms recipient is correctly Saibu rather than an unsupported third person.
