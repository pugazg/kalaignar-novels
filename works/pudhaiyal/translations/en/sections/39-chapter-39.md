# 39

The kudukuduppai fortune-teller came inside with a smiling face and sat where Ambalam indicated.

“Hey, Kudukuduppandi—what is this good time you say is coming? Tell me a little more clearly!”

“Am I the one saying it? Sakthi Mahakali—Bhagavathi says it! Ayya will receive all eight kinds of wealth. The man who thinks evil of Ayya will go crying ‘Ayyo!’ He will go crying ‘Ayyo!’”

“Hey, don't make noise! Answer what I ask!… You said Ayya is struggling over some matter. What matter is it? Tell me and let us see!”

“Even at dawn I told it. Ayya worries whether the matter he is striving over will ripen or remain unripe. Ayya need not worry. It is something concerning the earth—that is why Ayya is struggling. Without further struggle there will be victory! The matter will succeed!”

“Let victory be. Tell me the name of the matter. What does ‘something concerning the earth’ mean?”

“Bhagavathi speaks only briefly! She will not give so much detail! Something concerning the earth—something concerning the soil!”

“Then will I buy land?”

“Yes, Ayya will buy a field—a golden field! He will buy it happily! Saibu asks for a torn cloth, a skirt, something. Give him something. Ayya looks like a Maharaja!”

“Will I buy a golden field? Hey, will I? Will I get it? Tell me that!”

“Bhagavathi says you will get it! Even at dawn I said Lakshmi is coming to this house!”

“What will I get from under the earth? Treasure?”

“Bhagavathi says it is something connected with gold that you will get! She says you will get it soon too!”

“Is that so?”

“Ayya has placed his faith in one place and is wandering after it! Ayya will not succeed in that place! Bhagavathi says the place where Ayya will succeed is somewhere else!”

“Which place? Which place?”

“Another wretched fellow is going to try for the very thing Ayya is trying for. At the proper moment he will be caught!”

“Going to be caught? He has already been caught!”

“Not the man Ayya means—the man Saibu means. A Chandalan old enough to die! He is going to go crying ‘Ayyo,’ Amma Bhagavathi says! Even at dawn Saibu said it. With your golden hand, toss Saibu a torn cloth or skirt, saying ‘Here!’, Maharaja—good times are being born!”

“An old man is thinking treachery against me? Here, Saibu! Think calmly and tell me. Who is he?”

The fortune-teller thought for a moment and then looked at Ambalam's face and began speaking rapidly.

“Saibu thought! Bhagavathi says: an old man—a deceitful man—has the property Ayya is searching for! He is going to dig it up—but Bhagavathi says **it** will not remain in his hand; it will come only into Ayya's hand!”
