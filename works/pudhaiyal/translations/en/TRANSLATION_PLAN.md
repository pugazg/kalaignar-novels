# English Translation Plan — புதையல்

Working English title: **Treasure**

The Tamil title `புதையல்` remains the authoritative archival title.

## Authority hierarchy

1. canonical verified Tamil `../../pages/` records;
2. checked assembled Tamil `../../sections/` layer;
3. `../../audit.md`, page map and source metadata;
4. external knowledge only for separately marked editorial context, never to alter source wording.

If assembled Tamil and canonical pages differ, canonical pages govern until the Tamil archival layer is corrected from source evidence.

## Split-part workflow

Translation may proceed incrementally after a split receives a part-level Tamil audit pass under `../../PART_COMPLETION_WORKFLOW.md`.

A split-PDF boundary is not a narrative boundary. Translation batches may carry provenance markers for part boundaries, but may not invent chapter/scene breaks. Cross-split words, sentences and chapters must remain continuous.

A translated split may be marked `english-part-reviewed` after bilingual source checking. Whole-work English `verified` remains blocked until the full source is known and the final bilingual review passes.

## Style

- clear, readable, source-bound English;
- no summarising or literary rewriting;
- preserve every substantive sentence, dialogue line, repetition and rhetorical turn;
- preserve satire, humour, colloquial contrast, violence, romance and social/political force without softening or intensifying;
- do not convert source uncertainty or folklore into externally asserted fact;
- preserve source-specific oddity rather than inventing a smoother Tamil underneath it;
- maintain page/scan traceability through comments.

## Names and places

Use stable conservative transliteration, while leaving the Tamil canonical layer untouched.

Initial forms:

- `வெள்ளியம்பலம்` → **Velliyambalam**
- `மருங்கப் பள்ளம் / மருங்கப்பள்ளம்` → **Marungappallam**
- `சாளுவ நாயக்கன் பட்டினம்` → **Saluva Nayakkan Pattinam**
- `அதிராம்பட்டினம்` → **Adirampattinam**
- `புதுப்பட்டினம்` → **Puduppattinam**
- `பேராவூரணி` → **Peravurani**
- `நாடியம்` → **Nadiyam**
- `மனோரா` → **Manora**
- `சரபோஜி` → **Sarabhoji**

Where the source itself discusses a name's derivation, do not erase that discussion by silently substituting a modern standardized place-name.

## Difficult / culture-specific terms

Prefer conservative retention plus glossary where a precise English replacement would overstate certainty.

Initial decisions:

- `பொன் குட்டம் / பொன் குட்டக் கல்` → **pon-kuttam / pon-kuttam stone** on first occurrence with concise contextual explanation from the source itself; do not invent a mineralogical identification.
- `கம்மாளர்` → **Kammalar** in the inscription/riddle unless later context requires a narrower rendering; source itself asks whether the term means only goldsmiths, so the translation must preserve that uncertainty.
- `பாண்டு` → transliterate **pandu** where the source names the ailment historically; do not diagnose it from modern medicine.

## Dialogue / punctuation

Follow the audited Tamil's dialogue sequence and speaker ambiguity. English punctuation may be made readable, but no speaker label may be invented when the source does not supply one.

## Review states

- `draft-translated`
- `source-checked`
- `reviewed`
- part checkpoint: `english-part-reviewed`
- whole work: `verified` only after final complete-source bilingual review.

## Whole-work gate

No `RELEASE_REPORT.md` verdict and no claim of whole-work English verification until the true source ending/back matter, complete Tamil page map, final Tamil audit and final bilingual review all pass.
