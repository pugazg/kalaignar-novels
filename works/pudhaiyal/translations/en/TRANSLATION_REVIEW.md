## Part-005 correction addendum — PASS

### Scan 215 / printed 213

Tamil source corrections:

- `பாமளாவும்` → `பரிமளாவும்`;
- `கொழுந்தே` → `கொழுந்தாம்`;
- `என்று தழுதழுத்த குரலிலே கேட்டான்` → `என்று தழுதழுத்த குரலிலே அவன் கேட்டான்`.

English review result:

- **Pamala** corrected to **Parimala**;
- the earlier vocative reading of `கொழுந்தே` was removed; the corrected line reads **“I am the father who begot you, the shoot of my clan—”**;
- the added Tamil subject `அவன்` requires no new English semantic repair because the English already says **“he asked”**.

### Scan 216 / printed 214

Tamil source corrections:

- `புண்ணுக்கு மருந்து கேட்கிறாய்.` → `புண்ணுக்கு மருந்து கேள்; தருகிறேன்.`;
- `அவன் வீறுகொண்டு` → `அவன் வீரனாக`.

English review result:

- **“Durai! Ask for medicine for the wound; I'll give it. Do not ask me to pierce it with a spear. I cannot!”**;
- **“But he did not stand as a warrior and fight.”**

These are genuine semantic/fidelity refinements and are now incorporated in Chapter 24 English.
