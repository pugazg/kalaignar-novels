# Old-glyph correction — Pudhaiyal scans 246 and 248

Date: 2026-08-30

This note records a correction to the first Part-006 native-fidelity pass.

The initial review misread the old-style printed final `லை` glyph in several `வில்லை` endings as if the final vowel-bearing letter were absent. High-resolution reinspection of the native source, prompted by the user, confirms that the source actually prints the full forms below.

## Scan 246 / printed 242

Confirmed from native pixels:

- `தெரியவில்லை` — **source reading**;
- `தாளவில்லை` — **source reading**.

The earlier repository readings `தெரியவில்ல` and `தாளவில்ல` were assistant misreadings of the old glyph and are withdrawn.

The separate source correction `அந்த இருட்டில-` → `அந்த இருட்டிலே —` remains valid.

## Scan 248 / printed 244

The same old-glyph pattern was rechecked rather than assuming the earlier interpretation was safe.

Confirmed from native pixels:

- `புரியவில்லை` — **source reading**;
- `திரும்பி வரவில்லை` — **source reading**.

The earlier repository readings `புரியவில்ல` and `திரும்பி வரவில்ல` are withdrawn.

## Method lesson

For this edition, an old / faint final `லை` form can visually resemble a bare `ல்` at ordinary zoom. Future fidelity passes must enlarge such endings character-by-character before treating a baseline `வில்லை` as a discrepancy.

No grammar- or context-based normalization is involved here; these corrections are based on the native printed glyphs themselves.
