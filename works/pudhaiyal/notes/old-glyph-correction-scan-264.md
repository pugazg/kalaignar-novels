## Permanent lesson

The historical-typeform safeguard must not focus only on `லை` / final `ை`. **Any Tamil vowel sign may be faint or visually merged into an older glyph form.** In this source, scan 264 demonstrates that `ா` can be missed at ordinary zoom and falsely shorten `என்னா` to `என்ன`.

Before overriding a baseline by deleting or changing any vowel sign:

1. inspect the whole glyph cluster at high resolution;
2. perform a second independent pass;
3. compare same-edition typeforms when useful;
4. require positive native-pixel evidence;
5. if still ambiguous, retain the baseline and `needs-review`.
